(Draft v1) 2027 Objectives related to Pillar 2 (Adoption & Utility)

Driving widespread, non-speculative utility by focusing on high-value industry verticals, superior user experience (UX), and enterprise-grade security.

To share your feedback:

Open a PR in github https://github.com/IntersectMBO/product-website/tree/master/docs/2027-objectives

A.1. High-Value Verticals​

Drive real-world adoption by focusing on high-value sectors including DeFi, real-world assets, supply chains, and payments. Enable secure financial services, asset tokenization, transparent traceability, and fast, low-cost transactions.

Add between 1 to 3 objectives (add all 3 only if it is really needed)

Objective — What should improve in 2027?Why does it matter?How could we assess progress?DomainOther supported Pillars
Objective 1 — DeFi Competitiveness & LiquidityIncrease the competitiveness of Cardano's DeFi ecosystem by improving access to sustainable liquidity, reducing the cost of trading and lending, and attracting greater participation from users and external capital providers.Competitive financial services and deep, sustainable liquidity are essential to attracting users, builders and external capital. Without them, trading and borrowing can remain less attractive than on other ecosystems, limiting economic activity and adoption. Improving DeFi competitiveness also supports Cardano's long-term TVL and protocol revenue goals.DeFi becomes more competitive (progress): Price impact and total execution costs for benchmark trades; borrowing costs compared with leading ecosystems. Liquidity becomes more sustainable (progress): TVL and liquidity retained 90 days after incentives end. External capital participation increases (progress): Amount and share of liquidity provided by external capital providers. Stablecoin adoption increases (progress): Stablecoin supply, transfer volumes and recurring utilisation.Adoption & Ecosystem GrowthPillar 1 — I.1 Scalability & Interoperability (cross-chain liquidity and interoperability). Pillar 5 — E.1 Financial Stewardship & Tokenomics (treasury capital deployment and protocol revenue).
Objective 2 — Institutional & Enterprise AdoptionIncrease real-world adoption of Cardano by enabling more institutions, businesses and public bodies to deploy and sustain production services, supporting recurring on-chain activity and bringing new assets and economic value into the ecosystem.Institutional and enterprise adoption can generate recurring, non-speculative demand through payments, asset tokenisation, supply chains and financial services. Moving beyond pilots into sustained production use demonstrates practical utility, attracts external capital and strengthens Cardano's economic activity.More organisations use Cardano in production (progress): Number of institutions, businesses and public bodies operating live services, including conversions from pilots. Recurring economic activity increases (progress): Transactions, value transferred and sustained usage generated by production services. Real-world asset adoption increases (progress): Value and utilisation of assets issued on Cardano. External investment increases (progress): Net new non-treasury capital committed or deployed, including capital retained after initial incentives.Adoption & Ecosystem GrowthPillar 2 — A.2 Experience (enterprise identity, privacy, security and compliance). Pillar 4 — C.2 Global Engagement & Market Adoption (market access and qualified commercial opportunities).
Objective 3 — Emerging Adoption OpportunitiesExpand Cardano's adoption potential by identifying and validating emerging use cases and high-value sectors capable of generating sustained, real-world usage and economic activity.New opportunities can emerge from changing market conditions, technology developments and unmet industry needs. Validating promising use cases allows Cardano to diversify its sources of adoption and prioritise future resources based on evidence of real demand and economic potential.New adoption opportunities are validated (progress): Number of emerging sectors or use cases with credible evidence of demand. Early adoption is demonstrated (progress): Recurring users, transactions or economic activity generated by supported use cases. Commercial viability is demonstrated (progress): Evidence of customer demand, continued usage or external willingness to invest. Promising opportunities are identified for further growth (progress): Validated use cases with sufficient evidence to justify additional investment.Adoption & Ecosystem GrowthPillar 4 — C.2 Global Engagement & Market Adoption (market discovery and engagement). Other pillars depending on the specific use case.

A.2. Experience (Business & Consumer)​

Simplify Cardano's user experience by reducing technical complexity and making blockchain interactions seamless for consumers and businesses. Integrate decentralized identity, privacy, security, and compliance capabilities to support enterprise adoption.

Add between 1 to 3 objectives (add all 3 only if it is really needed)

Objective — What should improve in 2027?Why does it matter?How could we assess progress?DomainOther supported Pillars
Objective 1 — User Experience & AccessibilityReduce the complexity and friction of onboarding, transacting and accessing Cardano-based services, making them more accessible and competitive for consumers and businesses.Complex onboarding, unfamiliar transaction processes and fragmented experiences discourage adoption even when applications provide genuine value. Improving usability and accessibility can increase conversion, reduce abandonment and support sustained use of Cardano-based services.Onboarding becomes easier (progress): Time, steps and successful completion rates for common onboarding journeys. Transactions become easier to complete (progress): Time, cost and user effort required for common activities. User experience improves (progress): User satisfaction, task success and abandonment rates. Users continue using Cardano services (progress): Retention following initial onboarding.Adoption & Ecosystem GrowthPillar 2 — A.1 High-Value Verticals (increased adoption of applications and services). Pillar 2 — A.3 Developer Experience (tools and integrations enabling better application experiences).
Objective 2 — Enterprise ReadinessReduce barriers to enterprise and institutional adoption by improving the availability and accessibility of identity, privacy, security and compliance capabilities within Cardano-based services.Businesses and regulated institutions often require identity, privacy, security and compliance capabilities before adopting blockchain-based services. Improving access to these capabilities reduces integration barriers, strengthens confidence and makes Cardano more suitable for enterprise use cases.Enterprise capabilities become more accessible (progress): Availability and adoption of identity, privacy, security and compliance capabilities within production services. Integration becomes easier (progress): Reduction in time and effort required to meet relevant enterprise requirements. More services meet enterprise needs (progress): Number of Cardano-based services meeting documented institutional security and compliance requirements.Adoption & Ecosystem GrowthPillar 2 — A.1 High-Value Verticals (institutional and enterprise adoption). Pillar 1 — I.1 Scalability & Interoperability (underlying technical capabilities and interoperability).

A.3. Developer Experience​

Strengthen the developer ecosystem through sustainable open-source tooling, frameworks, and infrastructure. Improve developer onboarding, education, and compatibility with existing blockchain tools to reduce barriers to building on Cardano.

Add between 1 to 3 objectives (add all 3 only if it is really needed)

Objective — What should improve in 2027?Why does it matter?How could we assess progress?DomainOther supported Pillars
Objective 1 — Developer AccessibilityReduce the time, cost and complexity of building and deploying applications on Cardano, making development more accessible to both existing Cardano developers and those coming from other ecosystems.Development complexity and limited compatibility with familiar tools can discourage builders and slow application development. Improving onboarding, tooling and development workflows can attract developers, accelerate delivery and make Cardano more competitive as an application platform.Developer onboarding becomes easier (progress): Time to first successful application deployment. Development becomes more efficient (progress): Time and effort required for common development tasks. Compatibility improves (progress): Support for widely adopted development tools and frameworks. Developer experience improves (progress): Developer satisfaction and reported barriers to development.Developer SupportPillar 4 — C.1 Talent Acquisition & Retention (attracting and retaining skilled developers). Pillar 2 — A.1 High-Value Verticals (enabling development of applications supporting real-world adoption).
Objective 2 — Sustainable Developer InfrastructureImprove the reliability, accessibility and long-term sustainability of Cardano's essential developer tools, libraries and infrastructure to support continued application development and maintenance.Developers depend on reliable, maintained and accessible tools to build applications efficiently. Unsupported dependencies, fragmented infrastructure and inadequate maintenance increase development costs and risks, discouraging sustained ecosystem participation.Essential developer tools become more reliable (progress): Reliability and maintenance status of widely used tools and libraries. Maintenance gaps decrease (progress): Reduction in unsupported dependencies and critical unresolved issues. Developer tooling adoption increases (progress): Usage of maintained SDKs, libraries and frameworks. Developer satisfaction improves (progress): Reported quality, accessibility and reliability of developer infrastructure.Developer SupportPillar 1 — I.1 Scalability & Interoperability (dependencies on core protocol and infrastructure). Pillar 4 — C.1 Talent Acquisition & Retention (supporting long-term developer participation).
