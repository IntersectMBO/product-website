(Draft v1) 2027 Objectives related to Pillar 4 (Community & Ecosystem Growth)

Driving global engagement through a market-centric approach, cultivating a skilled developer base, and proactively demonstrating ecosystem value.

To share your feedback:

Open a PR in github https://github.com/IntersectMBO/product-website/tree/master/docs/2027-objectives

C.1. Talent Acquisition & Retention​

Develop a sustainable talent pipeline through educational initiatives, practical learning opportunities, and hands-on experience with Cardano technology. Support long-term contributor retention by connecting emerging talent with structured ecosystem funding opportunities.

Add between 1 to 3 objectives (add all 3 only if it is really needed)

Objective — What should improve in 2027?Why does it matter?How could we assess progress?DomainOther supported Pillars
Objective 1 — Talent Acquisition & DevelopmentIncrease the number of skilled technical and non-technical contributors entering the Cardano ecosystem and progressing from initial engagement to meaningful, sustained participation in ecosystem projects and organisations.Cardano's growth depends on attracting and developing capable contributors across technical, commercial and other specialised roles. Education and outreach only generate lasting value when participants acquire relevant skills and progress into meaningful ecosystem activity. A stronger talent pipeline increases the ecosystem's capacity to innovate, develop applications and support adoption.More people progress into active contribution (progress): Share of participants in education, hackathons and outreach initiatives who subsequently contribute to Cardano projects or organisations. Contributors remain active (progress): Proportion remaining active 6–12 months after initial engagement. Practical skills improve (progress): Participants demonstrating relevant capabilities through projects, employment or other meaningful contributions. The talent pipeline expands (progress): Growth in active technical, commercial and specialised contributors, including in priority regions.Adoption & Ecosystem GrowthPillar 2 — A.3 Developer Experience (reducing barriers for developers entering the ecosystem). Pillar 2 — A.1 High-Value Verticals (availability of talent supporting real-world applications).
Objective 2 — Builder & Contributor RetentionImprove the retention and long-term sustainability of active Cardano contributors and ecosystem teams by increasing their opportunities to secure the resources, connections and support needed to continue developing and growing within the ecosystem.Retaining experienced contributors and established teams preserves knowledge, development capacity and ecosystem continuity. Limited access to funding, customers and partnerships can prevent promising teams from sustaining their work, even after successful launches. Improving access to meaningful opportunities supports long-term ecosystem participation and growth.More contributors and teams remain active (progress): Year-on-year retention of active contributors and ecosystem teams. Teams become more sustainable (progress): Share of teams remaining active beyond initial funding or support. Access to growth opportunities improves (progress): Teams securing follow-on funding, external capital, commercial opportunities or meaningful partnerships. Experienced contributors remain in the ecosystem (progress): Retention of teams and individuals with demonstrated ongoing activity or traction.Adoption & Ecosystem GrowthPillar 2 — A.1 High-Value Verticals (commercial adoption of applications). Pillar 2 — A.3 Developer Experience (tools and infrastructure enabling continued development).
Objective 3 — Awareness & CredibilityStrengthen awareness, understanding and credibility of Cardano among relevant businesses, institutions, developers and other target audiences by demonstrating its technological capabilities and proven ecosystem value.Potential users, businesses, institutions and developers need to understand Cardano's capabilities and the value it can deliver. Greater awareness and credibility can improve how Cardano is perceived, increase its consideration as a technology platform and support long-term ecosystem growth. Evidence-based communication also helps distinguish real achievements from speculative narratives.Target audience awareness increases (progress): Changes in recognition of Cardano among relevant audiences. Understanding of Cardano improves (progress): Evidence that target audiences better understand Cardano's capabilities and use cases. Credibility and consideration improve (progress): Changes in perception, trust and willingness to consider Cardano. Relevant market visibility increases (progress): Share of voice in relevant industries and engagement with verified ecosystem achievements.Marketing, Analysts & EventsPillar 2 — A.1 High-Value Verticals (demonstrating real-world adoption). Pillar 2 — A.2 Experience (communicating the accessibility and capabilities of Cardano-based services).

C.2. Global Engagement & Market Adoption​

Strengthen Cardano's global presence by communicating its technological strengths and demonstrating tangible ecosystem value. Drive adoption through targeted regional partnerships, localized engagement, and market-specific strategies.

Add between 1 to 3 objectives (add all 3 only if it is really needed)

Objective — What should improve in 2027?Why does it matter?How could we assess progress?DomainOther supported Pillars
Objective 1 — Market Access & EngagementIncrease Cardano's engagement with relevant markets, businesses and institutions by expanding access to potential customers, partners and decision-makers, creating more qualified opportunities for ecosystem adoption and growth.Cardano needs to reach the organisations and markets where its technology can solve meaningful problems. Targeted engagement with relevant industries, regions and decision-makers can create commercial opportunities, strengthen partnerships and help ecosystem teams reach potential customers. Measuring outcomes rather than event attendance also improves the effectiveness of ecosystem outreach.Qualified market engagement increases (progress): Number and relevance of potential customers, partners and decision-makers engaged. More commercial opportunities emerge (progress): Qualified business and partnership opportunities generated through ecosystem engagement. Opportunities progress towards adoption (progress): Share progressing to substantive discussions, evaluations or pilots. Ecosystem teams gain market access (progress): Teams accessing relevant potential customers, partners or commercial opportunities.Marketing, Analysts & EventsPillar 2 — A.1 High-Value Verticals (converting opportunities into sustained institutional and commercial adoption). Pillar 4 — C.1 Talent Acquisition & Retention (commercial opportunities supporting ecosystem team sustainability).
Objective 2 — Ecosystem Visibility & Data TransparencyImprove the accuracy, accessibility and comparability of Cardano's ecosystem data, enabling external audiences to reliably assess its adoption, economic activity and performance relative to other blockchain ecosystems.Businesses, institutions, investors and developers rely on credible information when evaluating blockchain ecosystems. Incomplete or inconsistent reporting can prevent Cardano's actual capabilities and activity from being properly understood. Better data coverage, transparent measurements and comparability strengthen ecosystem credibility and support informed decisions.Ecosystem data coverage improves (progress): Share of significant Cardano applications and activities represented on major analytics platforms. Reporting gaps decrease (progress): Reduction in missing or inconsistent ecosystem information. Core metrics become more consistent (progress): Alignment of definitions and methodologies for TVL, monthly active users and transactions across relevant platforms. External visibility improves (progress): Availability of reliable Cardano data in widely used industry research, dashboards and comparative analysis.Marketing, Analysts & EventsPillar 2 — A.1 High-Value Verticals (visibility of actual adoption and economic activity). Pillar 5 — E.1 Financial Stewardship & Tokenomics (visibility of economic performance and network revenue). All pillars (consistent measurement of 2030 strategic KPIs).
Objective 3 — Awareness & CredibilityStrengthen awareness, understanding and credibility of Cardano among relevant businesses, institutions, developers and other target audiences by demonstrating its technological capabilities and proven ecosystem value.Potential users, businesses, institutions and developers need to understand Cardano's capabilities and the value it can deliver. Greater awareness and credibility can improve how Cardano is perceived, increase its consideration as a technology platform and support long-term ecosystem growth. Evidence-based communication also helps distinguish real achievements from speculative narratives.Target audience awareness increases (progress): Changes in recognition of Cardano among relevant audiences. Relevant market visibility increases (progress): Share of voice in relevant industries and engagement with verified ecosystem achievements.Marketing, Analysts & EventsPillar 2 — A.1 High-Value Verticals (demonstrating real-world adoption).
