(Draft) 2027 Objectives related to Pillar 3 (Governance)

Cardano governance must be hard to capture, easy to use, and paced. This builds directly on Cardano’s tripartite model of DReps, Constitutional Committee, and SPOs.

To share your feedback:

Open a PR in github https://github.com/IntersectMBO/product-website/tree/master/docs/2027-objectives

G.1. Incentivized & Accessible Governance

Encourage broad and meaningful governance participation through activity-based incentives for DReps, SPOs, and Constitutional Committee members. Improve accessibility through tools that simplify delegation, voting, proposal submission, and communication.

Objective 1 — Accessible & Reliable Governance ParticipationImprove the accessibility, reliability and usability of Cardano's governance participation mechanisms, enabling DReps, SPOs and ADA holders to participate effectively in governance without unnecessary technical or operational barriers.Broad and meaningful governance participation depends on reliable, accessible mechanisms for delegation, voting, proposal submission and communication. Technical complexity, unavailable tools or inadequate maintenance can discourage participation and concentrate governance activity among technically experienced stakeholders.Essential governance functions remain accessible (achievement): Availability of core registration, delegation, voting and governance action functions through maintained tools. Governance participation becomes easier (progress): Time, steps and successful completion rates for common activities. Governance services become more reliable (progress): Availability, continuity and maintenance coverage of essential governance tools. Objective 2 — Meaningful & Distributed Governance ParticipationIncrease meaningful and consistent participation in Cardano governance while improving the distribution of effective voting power and reducing dependence on a small number of influential governance participants.Cardano's governance resilience depends on diverse and active participation. Concentrated voting power and inconsistent engagement can weaken representation, increase governance capture risks and reduce confidence in collective decision-making. More meaningful participation and a healthier distribution of voting power strengthen the legitimacy and resilience of decentralised governance.Governance participation becomes more consistent (progress): Share of active voting power participating regularly in governance actions. Voting power becomes more distributed (progress): Number of DReps required to control a majority of effective voting power. Participation becomes more diverse (progress): Number and distribution of active DReps, including smaller representatives. Meaningful engagement increases (progress): Consistency of participation and publication of voting rationales.Governance CoordinationPillar 4 — C.1 Talent Acquisition & Retention (developing and retaining capable governance contributors). Pillar 4 — C.2 Global Engagement & Market Adoption (governance awareness and community engagement).

G.2. Turnout-Aware Voting with Delegator Safeguard

Introduce voting mechanisms that account for participation levels to ensure governance decisions reflect meaningful community support. Strengthen delegator protections by enabling ADA holders to override DRep votes on individual Layer 2 decisions without changing delegation.

G.3. Treasury Seasons

Establish structured annual governance cycles for treasury and budget proposals to improve coordination and decision-making. Increase transparency through regular reporting while reducing proposal overload and governance fatigue.

Add between 1 to 3 objectives (add all 3 only if it is really needed)

Objective 1 — Effective & Transparent Treasury GovernanceImprove the predictability, coordination and transparency of Cardano's treasury governance by establishing more structured funding cycles that support informed decision-making, public accountability and effective participation.Predictable and transparent treasury governance enables participants to plan, evaluate and make funding decisions more effectively. Better coordination can reduce duplicated efforts, proposal overload and governance fatigue, while improving accountability for treasury resources and confidence in funding decisions.Treasury governance becomes more predictable (progress): Publication of and adherence to a clearly communicated treasury governance calendar. Additional Section: Institutional Continuity

Keep Cardano’s governance institutions working without interruption: a Constitutional Committee renewed on time, and a Constitution that can be amended through a clear, published process. Not a Cardano 2030 Strategy section today; proposed as the foundation G.1 and G.3 depend on.

Add between 1 to 3 objectives (add all 3 only if it is really needed)

Objective — What should improve in 2027?Why does it matter?How could we assess progress?DomainOther supported Pillars
Objective 1 - Constitutional Committee ContinuityMaintain the continuity and effective functioning of Cardano's Constitutional Committee by ensuring timely, transparent and legitimate renewal of its membership in accordance with constitutional requirements.The Constitutional Committee is a core institution in Cardano's governance system. Failure to maintain its constitutionally required membership could disrupt constitutional governance and the processing of important decisions.Required membership is maintained: Constitutional Committee membership remains compliant with applicable requirements. Renewal is completed on time: Required elections and committee updates are completed before relevant membership expiry. Renewal is transparent and verifiable: Election and appointment processes follow applicable procedures and provide publicly accessible results.Governance CoordinationPillar 3 — G.1 Incentivized & Accessible Governance (institutional participation and governance continuity).
Objective 2 - Constitutional Amendment ReadinessMaintain an accessible, transparent and reliable process for proposing, reviewing and deciding constitutional amendments, enabling Cardano's governance system to respond effectively to evolving needs.Cardano must be able to evolve its constitutional framework when required. Unavailable or unclear amendment processes could delay important governance decisions and create dependencies for protocol development.Amendment processes remain available: Clearly documented and accessible procedures are maintained. Proposals can be processed effectively: Submitted amendments can progress through the applicable procedures. Processes remain transparent: Proposals, consultations and decisions are publicly accessible. Avoidable delays are minimised: No material delays attributable to missing procedural coordination.Governance CoordinationPillar 3 — G.1 Incentivized & Accessible Governance (accessible governance processes). Pillar 3 — G.3 Treasury Seasons (procedural coordination where relevant).
Objective 3 - Governance Coordination ContinuityMaintain the operational capacity and coordination necessary for Cardano's governance institutions, committees and decision-making processes to function reliably, transparently and without avoidable interruption.Governance institutions depend on ongoing coordination, facilitation and administrative support. Without operational continuity, essential activities may be interrupted, weakening the effectiveness and reliability of Cardano governance.Essential coordination remains available: Core governance support functions operate without material interruption. Governance processes are delivered: Required meetings, elections and decision-making processes are supported when needed. Operational accountability is maintained: Public reporting on coordination activities and expenditure. Continuity is preserved: No material disruption caused by inadequate operational support.Governance CoordinationPillar 3 — G.1 Incentivized & Accessible Governance. Pillar 3 — G.3 Treasury Seasons.
