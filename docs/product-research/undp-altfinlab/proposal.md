**COVER LETTER **

Dear CPC Committee,

UNDP AltFinLab is pleased to submit this proposal under RFP-05: Government and Emerging Markets Entry Strategy.

This proposal builds on UNDP AltFinLab's ongoing work supporting governments, public institutions, UNDP Country Offices, and the broader blockchain ecosystem in exploring responsible blockchain adoption and implementation pathways through initiatives including the Government Blockchain Academy (GBA), the SDG Blockchain Accelerator, the Blockchain Academy, the UNDP Blockchain Advisory Group, and the UNDP Blockchain Impact Fund.

The proposal is uniquely positioned due to UNDP's direct engagement with governments through more than 170 Country Offices globally. These Country Offices work closely with ministries, regulators, public-sector agencies, and digital transformation stakeholders, providing operational visibility into institutional priorities, implementation barriers, infrastructure readiness, and public-sector innovation dynamics.

Importantly, the proposal is grounded in existing government engagement and implementation discussions already underway through the Government Blockchain Academy (GBA) with countries including Malaysia, El Salvador, Türkiye, Dominican Republic, Uzbekistan, and Argentina. The Academy provides a structured platform for ongoing collaboration with governments and ministries across emerging markets. Beyond blockchain education, the GBA supports governments through strategic guidance on priority public-sector use cases and potential pilot initiatives identified by participating ministries and institutions. Through onboarding workshops, live sessions, solution facilitation discussions, and post-Academy engagement, the GBA creates direct channels for understanding government priorities, implementation interests, operational constraints, and institutional readiness for blockchain adoption. As the Academy begins engaging governments, it will also generate additional real-time insights into how governments are evaluating blockchain technologies, which sectors and use cases are being prioritised, and what forms of infrastructure, policy support,

partnerships, and implementation pathways are required to move from education and exploration toward pilot development and operational deployment.

In addition, the SDG Blockchain Accelerator has supported more than 45 blockchain-enabled use cases aligned with sustainable development challenges, 23 of which have been building on Cardano infrastructure or related technologies. Several of these use cases involved governments, ministries, public-sector stakeholders, and national development programmes. These experiences provide valuable implementation insights into public-sector blockchain adoption realities, institutional readiness, infrastructure constraints, and operational deployment pathways, while also informing this proposal through practical lessons learned from supporting blockchain-enabled innovation across emerging markets.

Unlike traditional market-entry research focused primarily on ecosystem mapping or theoretical adoption analysis, this proposal combines:

●​ institutional insights from UNDP Country Offices that have worked closely with governments and ministries in these countries for many years, ●​ direct access to government insights across different ministries and public institutions, strengthened through interviews and one-on-one discussions with relevant stakeholders, ●​ operational implementation insights, ●​ comparative country assessments, ●​ and implementation-grounded strategic intelligence through Government Blockchain Academy and SDG Blockchain Accelertor.

The project aims to generate practical and actionable recommendations regarding:

●​ government adoption readiness, ●​ public-sector implementation pathways, ●​ emerging-market prioritisation, ●​ infrastructure and regulatory requirements, ●​ and responsible ecosystem engagement models.

We appreciate your consideration and look forward to contributing to this important research effort.

Sincerely,

UNDP Alternative Finance Lab (AltFinLab)

**1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY **

This proposal presents a government and emerging-markets entry strategy research initiative led by UNDP AltFinLab, leveraging the Government Blockchain Academy (GBA), SDG Blockchain Accelerator, and UNDP's global institutional network across more than 170 countries.

The project aims to generate practical and implementation-oriented strategic intelligence on how blockchain ecosystems can responsibly engage governments and public institutions across emerging markets.

The proposal is grounded in:

●​ direct government engagement, ●​ ministry-level discussions, ●​ existing public-sector blockchain exploration initiatives, ●​ and operational implementation insights.

The research builds on ongoing government discussions and blockchain engagement activities already underway in:

●​ Malaysia ●​ El Salvador ●​ Türkiye ●​ Dominican Republic ●​ Uzbekistan ●​ Argentina

Importantly, 23 use cases within the SDG Blockchain Accelerator have been building on Cardano infrastructure or related technologies, several of which involved governments, ministries, regulators,public-service delivery systems, and national development programmes.

The research will assess:

●​ government readiness, ●​ institutional feasibility, ●​ infrastructure maturity, ●​ regulatory environments, ●​ use-case suitability, ●​ procurement realities, ●​ and long-term adoption pathways.

**2. UNDERSTANDING OF THE BRIEF **

This proposal understands RFP-05 as a request for practical and evidence-based research focused on how blockchain ecosystems can responsibly and sustainably engage governments and public institutions across emerging markets.

The proposal recognises that government adoption depends not only on technology readiness, but also on:

●​ institutional trust, ●​ operational feasibility, ●​ procurement realities, ●​ regulatory alignment, ●​ implementation capacity, ●​ political prioritisation, ●​ infrastructure maturity, ●​ and stakeholder coordination.

The proposal therefore focuses on:

●​ identifying realistic government entry pathways, ●​ assessing implementation readiness, ●​ evaluating public-sector use-case suitability, ●​ understanding institutional barriers, ●​ and generating actionable recommendations for sustainable ecosystem engagement.

Unlike purely theoretical market analysis, the proposal is grounded in direct government engagement and operational implementation realities.

**Illustrative case:** Plastiks: plastic waste recovery and traceability. Through the SDG Blockchain Accelerator, AltFinLab supported Plastiks in developing a blockchain-based platform on Cardano that tokenises verified plastic recovery and links it to financing from corporate and philanthropic buyers.

The engagement moved from a single-country concept to active pilots with UNDP Country Offices in Armenia, El Salvador, India, and Zambia, with additional pilot funding secured from the Fondation Valery.

Lessons from this replication  particularly the institutional onboarding time required when moving between UNDP Country Offices with different operational priorities, and the importance of aligning blockchain-based MRV with existing national waste-management reporting systems directly inform the country-engagement sequencing and infrastructure-readiness assessment.

**15. RISK & BIAS MITIGATION PLAN **

Risks include:

●​ political shifts ●​ regulatory uncertainty ●​ institutional turnover ●​ ecosystem bias ●​ overestimation of blockchain suitability

Mitigation measures:

●​ technology-neutral methodology ●​ evidence-based analysis ●​ stakeholder validation ●​ comparative frameworks ●​ multi-source verification

Risk: researcher proximity to government counterparts. UNDP's embedded position with ministries is the proposal's central asset, but it also creates a risk that markets we are actively engaged in are scored more favourably than evidence warrants.

Mitigation: every country opportunity profile applies the same five-gate scoring rubric independent of UNDP engagement intensity; each use-case recommendation includes an explicit "is blockchain genuinely warranted vs. conventional systems" test (Section 9); and all final country recommendations are reviewed by at least one external technical reviewer from our team.** **

## 15. RISK & BIAS MITIGATION PLAN

Risks include:
- political shifts
- regulatory uncertainty
- institutional turnover
- ecosystem bias
- overestimation of blockchain suitability


Mitigation measures:
- technology-neutral methodology
- evidence-based analysis
- stakeholder validation
- comparative frameworks
- multi-source verification

Risk: researcher proximity to government counterparts. UNDP's embedded position with ministries is the proposal's central asset, but it also creates a risk that markets we are actively engaged in are scored more favourably than evidence warrants.

Mitigation: every country opportunity profile applies the same five-gate scoring rubric independent of UNDP engagement intensity; each use-case recommendation includes an explicit "is blockchain genuinely warranted vs. conventional systems" test (Section 9); and all final country recommendations are reviewed by at least one external technical reviewer from our team.
