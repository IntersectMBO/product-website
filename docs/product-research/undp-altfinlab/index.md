# UNDP AltFinLab

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **RFP** | RFP 05 — Government & Emerging Markets Entry Strategy |
| **Grant** | 41,400 ADA |

## Scope

Government adoption pathway research leveraging a 6-country blockchain academy network. Research maps regulatory and procurement landscapes, identifies high-opportunity government use cases for Cardano, and produces region-specific entry strategy recommendations.
