**Document Purpose**

This is the public summary of our draft Negative-Case and Non-Cardano Evidence Summary (D7). The full document, with every case and its evidence, is published alongside it in redacted form.

**Main findings**

1. **Most builders on other chains never considered Cardano.** Seven of the nine non-Cardano builders reported did not evaluate it.
2. **Where a reason is given, it is rarely technical.** Builders chose other chains for users and attention, sponsorship, developer experience and low fees. Only one named a missing Cardano capability: default privacy.
3. **Cardano deployments stall on money, regulation and sustainability, not throughput.** None of the stalled or ended cases we found was caused by a lack of Layer 2 capability.
4. **Funded integrations have mostly not changed builder plans.** Seven builders and providers said they made no difference; two said they did.
5. **Some peer approaches do not transfer.** Browser-embedded distribution depended on an existing installed base Cardano lacks. Conditioning grants on usage does transfer.

**What this means for decisions**

| Decision | What the evidence says |
|---|---|
| What not to fund | Layer 2 throughput as the answer to stalled African deployments; free services that compete with native paid providers without considering their survival |
| Where assumptions are weak | That builders would choose Cardano if it had the capability; that funded integrations change builder plans; that funding at proposal stage predicts lasting operation |
| Where peer approaches do not transfer | Browser-embedded distribution without a distribution partner; near-zero fees without protocol or wallet changes |

All findings are Medium or Low confidence.

**Limitations**

- Non-Cardano respondents are early-stage builders, so their choices show how chains are selected, not demand at scale.
- No large operator from the desk research list was interviewed.
