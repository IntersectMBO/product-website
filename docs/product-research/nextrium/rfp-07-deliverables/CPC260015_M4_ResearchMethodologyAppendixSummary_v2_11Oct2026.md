**Document Purpose**

This is the public summary of our Research Methodology Appendix (D11). The full appendix is published alongside it in redacted form.

**How the research was done**

- **Design:** desk research and screening (Milestone 1), 31 interviews across four respondent categories (Milestone 2), classification and drafting (Milestone 3), all mapped to RFP 07's eleven decision gates.
- **Respondents:** 15 African builders and operators on Cardano, 5 infrastructure and tooling teams, 1 bridge and partner-chain provider, and 10 non-Cardano or negative-case respondents. 25 chose named attribution, 4 anonymised and 2 confidential.
- **Instruments:** a screener and five category interview guides, published at Milestone 1. Interviews only; no survey.
- **Analysis:** 500 evidence extracts, 105 seven-alternative Non-Layer 2 tests, and 114 classified barriers, each traceable to its source.
- **Proprietary data:** none used.
- **Confidence:** High, Medium or Low on one rubric. Provider-side findings are held at Low, and our own validation work cannot set any finding above Low.

**Main limitations**

- One bridge, interoperability or wallet provider was interviewed against a committed five to eight; no wallet provider was interviewed.
- Scale figures are self-reported and were not verified.
- Most Cardano builders interviewed chose Cardano by familiarity, not comparison, and were recruited largely through Cardano networks.
- Many findings rest on one respondent.
