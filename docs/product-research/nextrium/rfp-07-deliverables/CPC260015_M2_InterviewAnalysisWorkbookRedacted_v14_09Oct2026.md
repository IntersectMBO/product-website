**Redaction Rules**

This document reproduces all eight sheets of the Milestone 2 analysis workbook with the following controls applied, per the consent tiers recorded in the Interview Register and the identification-by-elimination test in the Interview Analysis Instructions (Section 9.2):

- **Named** respondents: name and organisation shown.
- **Anonymised** respondents (R-008, R-016, R-023, R-027): views shown; names, organisation and product names replaced with a category label; register notes withheld.
- **Confidential** respondents (R-012, R-032) and the Confidential extract E-083: statement text and interview dates withheld; IDs, categories and classifications retained so counts and methods remain auditable.
- No respondent email addresses, phone numbers or other contact details are held in the workbook; internal Drive file references and transcript locations are removed.
