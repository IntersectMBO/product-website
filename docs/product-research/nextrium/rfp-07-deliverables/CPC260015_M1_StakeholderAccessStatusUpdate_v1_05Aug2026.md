**Document Purpose**

This document is deliverable D-M1-06 under the grant agreement. It reports the stakeholder access position at the close of Milestone 1, covering respondent categories and targets, access pathways, outreach status, identified access risks and mitigation.

Primary research interviews are conducted during Milestone 2. This report establishes whether access sufficient to deliver the committed interview range has been secured or is on track, and discloses any category where it is not.

**1. Access Position Summary**

**1.1 Committed Interview Range**

The submitted proposal commits to 30 to 44 interviews across four respondent categories, per Proposal Section 5: Category 1, 15 to 20; Category 2, 6 to 10; Category 3, 5 to 8; Category 4, 4 to 6. These sub-ranges sum exactly to the committed total, and the figures in this report are tracked against them.

**1.2 Category Position**

| **Category** | **Description**                                                  | **Target** | **Candidates identified** | **Access position**                                          |
|--------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|------------|---------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1            | African informal economy builders and operators on Cardano       | 15 to 20   | Five access pathways      | Established through existing relationships                   |
| 2            | Infrastructure, tooling, identity and application protocol teams | 6 to 10    | 14                        | Mixed: direct access on four, structured outreach on ten     |
| 3            | Bridge, interoperability and wallet providers                    | 5 to 8     | 12                        | Structured outreach on all; weakest access category          |

**1.3 Candidate Coverage Against Target**

Candidate counts exceed targets in every category to absorb non-response. Category 2 carries 14 candidates against a target of 6 to 10, Category 3 carries 12 against 5 to 8, and Category 4 carries 8 against 4 to 6. Category 1 is recruited through five community and direct pathways rather than a fixed candidate list, reflecting the volume target. The larger candidate pools in Categories 2 and 3 reflect a deliberate over-recruitment decision recorded in Research Design Document version 1.1, sized above the committed target to buffer against non-response; the delivery target and the figures reported in this document remain the range committed in Proposal Section 5.

**2.2 Categories 2, 3 and 4**

Direct access is established for four Category 2 respondents through prior technical work: the oracle provider engaged during schema validation, the identity maintainer community engaged during identity stack validation, and the partner-chain developer community engaged during devnet work. The identity maintainer community additionally holds weekly public community calls, providing a defined and reliable access route.

All Category 3 respondents require structured outreach with no prior relationship. This is the weakest access position of the four categories and is treated as the principal access risk.

Category 4 access is mixed. Community networks in Africa provide routes to builders on Stellar, Polygon and other non-Cardano infrastructure. Structured outreach is required for ecosystem teams and for operators running their own Layer 1 infrastructure.

**3. Outreach Status**

**3.1 Outreach Programme**

Outreach materials were issued to the research team at the start of the outreach window, comprising the respondent list, the consent and ethics protocol, four category-specific outreach templates, the outreach priority and escalation protocol, and the consent tracking register.

Outreach proceeds in a defined sequence. Two respondents are sequenced ahead of all others on time-sensitivity grounds, and one further respondent is sequenced third on evidence-value grounds. The remainder proceed in parallel across categories.

**4. Access Risks and Mitigation**

**4.1 Identified Risks**

| **Risk**                                                    | **Assessment**                                                                                   | **Mitigation**                                                                                                               |
|-------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Category 3 confirmations fall below five                    | Highest access risk. No prior relationship exists with any Category 3 respondent                 | Twelve candidates against a target of five to eight. Immediate escalation if confirmations fall below five                   |
| A priority respondent becomes unreachable                   | One Category 2 respondent published a scheduled operational wind-down during the research period | Sequenced first in the outreach programme. Contact attempted at the earliest available date and logged regardless of outcome |
| Respondents decline citing the commissioning body           | Would indicate a systematic framing problem affecting a whole category                           | Escalation trigger requiring immediate notification. Outreach templates disclose the commissioning body openly               |
| Respondents request terms outside the three consent options | Cannot be accepted without approval                                                              | Interview does not proceed until the Lead Researcher has determined the position                                             |
| Provider status changes during the interview window         | Four provider status changes occurred during desk research                                       | Status confirmed at first contact. Organisations that have ceased operating are recorded as a finding                        |

**4.2 Disclosure Commitment**

Where access falls below target in any category, this is disclosed proactively rather than concealed through substitution. Provider-side findings will not be presented at medium or high confidence if Category 3 access falls below the lower target. Confidence levels in all downstream deliverables reflect actual access achieved.

**5. Ethics and Consent Position**

Every respondent is informed of the research purpose, the commissioning body and project number, and how their input may be used. Before any research question is asked, the consent disclosure statement is read in full. Respondents choose named attribution, anonymised attribution, or fully confidential treatment, and may change that choice after the interview.

No respondent is recorded without explicit verbal consent obtained separately from attribution consent. All consent decisions are logged in the Consent Tracking Register on the day of contact. Respondents primarily seeking funding for Layer 2 or interoperability work are flagged at the point of logging, and their claims are separated from independent demand evidence throughout analysis.

Raw interview notes, transcripts and research data are stored in a password-protected project documentation system accessible only to the research team, and retained for a minimum of twelve months following project completion.

**7. Assessment**

Access sufficient to deliver the committed interview range has been established in Categories 1, 2 and 4 through a combination of direct relationships, community networks and structured outreach against candidate counts that materially exceed targets.

Category 3 remains the principal access risk. No prior relationship exists with any respondent in that category, and the target of five to eight interviews depends entirely on structured outreach succeeding. Twelve candidates are listed against that target. If confirmations fall below five, provider-side findings will carry reduced confidence and this will be stated explicitly in the affected deliverables.
