**Document Purpose**

This is the Milestone 3 classification sheet of our interview analysis workbook. It classifies every Non-Layer 2 test from Milestone 2 (T-) and every barrier from the Milestone 1 desk research (DR-) using the [Technical and Commercial Blocker Taxonomy](./CPC260015_M3_TechnicalCommercialBlockerTaxonomy_v1_09Oct2026.md), and records where each is routed. It is published so that every test and barrier cited in the Milestone 3 drafts can be traced. The other sheets of the workbook, including the evidence extracts and the tests themselves, are published with Milestone 2 in the redacted [Interview Analysis Workbook](https://github.com/Nextrium/cpc-product-website/blob/milestone-2-deliverables/docs/product-research/nextrium/rfp-07-deliverables/CPC260015_M2_InterviewAnalysisWorkbookRedacted_v14_09Oct2026.md).

**Contents:** 114 rows (105 tests and 9 desk-research barriers).

**Redaction Rules**

The same rules as the Milestone 2 redacted workbook apply:

- **Named** respondents: shown as recorded.
- **Anonymised** respondents (R-008, R-016, R-023, R-027): identified by ID and category; product names and descriptions removed.
- **Confidential** respondents (R-012, R-032) and the Confidential extract E-083: blocker text, use case and impact withheld; IDs, type, routing and confidence retained so counts remain auditable.
- Internal working notes are not published.

**AI Use Disclaimer**

AI tools were used in classification, as logged in our AI Tool Disclosure Log (grant clause 4.2). The lead researcher has reviewed this work and remains responsible for its accuracy. Feedback is welcome, and the work will be updated, or defended where necessary.
