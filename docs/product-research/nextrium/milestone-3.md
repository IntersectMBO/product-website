## Summary

Milestone 3 turned the primary research into the full draft deliverable set. Every blocker from the interviews and desk research was classified against the barrier taxonomy (114 classified rows); every screened interoperability pathway received a final value-flow classification (13 pathways); the interoperability requirements register (14 requirements) and the provider and partner opportunity analysis (21 providers) were compiled; and all recommended actions were sequenced (22 actions). All nine contracted draft deliverables are complete, together with the further drafts committed in our proposal. Each draft answers its decision gates with a stated confidence level and links to the evidence behind it. In short: demand waiting on Layer 2 is real but narrow and not yet at scale, the barriers that limit adoption are mostly about access and cost, no interoperability pathway yet shows net-new value for Cardano, and no action is recommended for new funding on current evidence.

## Issues and Blockers

- **Category 3 access (research limitation, carried from Milestone 2).** One bridge, interoperability or wallet provider was interviewed against a committed 5 to 8. In line with the Milestone 1 commitment, provider-side findings and Decision Gates 6 to 9 are held at Low confidence in every draft.
- **Independent technical review pending.** Thirteen technically complex items are with Samir Idris for independent technical review. Affected entries are marked in each draft and will be updated at Milestone 4.
- **Adjacent RFP numbering.** Our proposal paired some adjacent RFP numbers with the wrong topics. The drafts use the Committee's published numbering; the topics are unchanged. This is noted in the Cross-RFP Handoff Memo.
- **AI use.** AI tools were used in classification and drafting, as logged in the AI Tool Disclosure Log (grant clause 4.2). The lead researcher has reviewed this work. Feedback is welcome, and the work will be updated, or defended where necessary.
