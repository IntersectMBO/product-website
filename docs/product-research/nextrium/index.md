# NexTrium

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **RFP** | RFP 07 — L2 Adoption & Interoperability Demand Study |
| **Grant** | 25,000 ADA |

## Scope

L2 adoption demand research focused on the African informal economy. Research analyses the barriers to Layer 2 adoption on Cardano and the demand for interoperability, with a specific lens on how L2 solutions can serve underbanked and informal-economy participants across African markets.
