## Summary

Milestone 2 delivered the primary research programme: 31 interviews across all four respondent categories, within the committed range of 30 to 44. Every interview was extracted into 500 evidence statements. Every blocker claim was put through the seven-alternative Non-Layer 2 test (103 tests). The two contracted deliverables are complete: an initial blocked-demand case register of 15 cases, 12 of which pass the Layer 2 or interoperability workflow test, and an early demand-signal review answering Decision Gates 1 and 4 at an early stage. A redacted, consent-aware version of the full analysis workbook is published so that every case and finding can be traced to its source evidence.

## Issues and Blockers

- **Submission timing.** Appendix A sets 4 September 2026 for Milestone 2. Interviews ran until 20 September, and the Committee asked teams to wait for one milestone to be approved before submitting the next. Milestone 1 received its approvals on 16 and 20 September 2026. This report is submitted after that approval.
- **Category 3 access (research limitation).** One bridge, interoperability or wallet provider was interviewed against a committed 5 to 8. This was identified as the principal access risk in the Milestone 1 Stakeholder Access Status Update, flagged in the Milestone 1 report, and raised in writing with the Committee. It is reported as found. Respondents from other categories have not been substituted to answer Category 3 questions. In line with the Milestone 1 commitment, provider-side findings and Decision Gates 6 to 8 are held at Low confidence.
- **Category balance.** Category 2 is one interview below its lower bound (5 against 6). Category 4 is four above its upper bound (10 against 6). The total of 31 is within the committed 30 to 44.
- **Finding flagged for Milestone 3.** Two respondents deliver USSD access on Cardano at the application layer. This qualifies a Milestone 1 desk-research finding, and it is reported in the Early Demand-Signal Review (Section 4.1) for resolution at Milestone 3 rather than corrected unilaterally.
- **AI use.** AI tools were used in transcription, extraction, classification and drafting, as logged in the workbook (grant clause 4.2). The lead researcher has reviewed this work. Given the number of text-based extracts, a manual review may not catch every issue. Feedback is welcome, and the work will be updated, or defended where necessary.
