## Summary

Milestone 2 delivered the primary research programme: 31 interviews across all four respondent categories, within the committed range of 30 to 44. Every interview was extracted into 500 evidence statements. Every blocker claim was put through the seven-alternative Non-Layer 2 test (103 tests). The two contracted deliverables are complete: an initial blocked-demand case register of 15 cases, 11 of which pass the Layer 2 or interoperability workflow test, and an early demand-signal review answering Decision Gates 1 and 4 at an early stage. A redacted, consent-aware version of the full analysis workbook is published so that every case and finding can be traced to its source evidence.

## Issues and Blockers

- **Category 3 access (research limitation).** One bridge, interoperability or wallet provider was interviewed against a committed 5 to 8. This was identified as the principal access risk in the Milestone 1 Stakeholder Access Status Update, flagged in the Milestone 1 report, and raised in writing with the Committee. It is reported as found. Respondents from other categories have not been substituted to answer Category 3 questions. In line with the Milestone 1 commitment, provider-side findings and Decision Gates 6 to 9 are held at Low confidence.
- **Category balance.** Category 2 is one interview below its lower bound (5 against 6). Category 4 is four above its upper bound (10 against 6). The total of 31 is within the committed 30 to 44.
- **Finding flagged for Milestone 3.** Two respondents deliver USSD access on Cardano at the application layer. This qualifies a Milestone 1 desk-research finding, and it is reported in the Early Demand-Signal Review (Section 4.1) for resolution at Milestone 3 rather than corrected unilaterally.
- **AI use.** AI tools were used in transcription, extraction, classification and drafting, as logged in the workbook (grant clause 4.2). The lead researcher has reviewed this work. Given the number of text-based extracts, a manual review may not catch every issue. Feedback is welcome, and the work will be updated, or defended where necessary.

## Plan for Next Milestone

Milestone 3 (Analysis and Draft Deliverables) will:

- classify all blockers across the barrier taxonomy;
- produce final value-flow classifications for every screened interoperability pathway;
- compile the interoperability requirements register and the provider and partner opportunity analysis;
- deliver the full draft set for Committee review: Draft L2 Barrier Analysis, Draft L2 Demand Map, Draft Interoperability Requirements Register, Draft Interoperability Value-Flow Assessment, Draft Provider and Partner Opportunity Analysis, Draft Investment Sequencing Recommendation, Draft Evidence Threshold Framework, Draft Cross-RFP Handoff Memo and Draft Executive Decision Memo.

The four non-Layer 2 cases in the register will be carried into the Cross-RFP Handoff Memo: Case-003, Case-007 and Case-008 to RFP 6, and Case-009 to RFP 2. Layer 2 cases that depend on base-layer throughput will be re-tested against the Leios timeline.
