# ASISA / Odufuwa

| Field | Value |

| **RFP** | RFP 05 — Government & Emerging Markets Entry Strategy |
| **Grant** | 32,500 ADA |

## Scope

Insurance-sector research with academic supervision. Research focuses on emerging market entry strategy through the lens of the insurance and financial services sector, examining how Cardano can serve as infrastructure for insurance products and related financial services in target markets.
