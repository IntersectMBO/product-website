## 1. Purpose, Taxonomy, and Access-Status Ground Truth

This document constitutes Deliverable 3 of Milestone 2 under RFP 05 (`CPC-26-0014` / `ER-0004b-25`), executing the Phase 2/3 Stakeholder Ecosystem Mapping defined in **Chapter 4 (`Sections 4.1–4.8`)**, **Decision Gate 3 (`Section 5.6`)**, and **Section 9.6** of the Milestone 1 Research Design Report. Building directly on the 14 inception-stage institutions documented in Milestone 1 Section 4.3, this register maps **63 named institutions** across the Nigerian insurance, regulatory, actuarial, agricultural, payments, digital-asset, beneficiary, and Cardano technical ecosystem.

### 1.1 Four-Archetype Structure (Milestone 1 Section 4.3)

To enforce **Bias Control 1 (Cardano Insider Optimism Separation)**, **Bias Control 3 (Northern and Southern Sampling)**, **Bias Control 5 (Event Sample Tagging)**, and **Bias Control 7 (Independent Corroboration of Partner Claims)**, the ecosystem map is structured across the four archetypes established in Milestone 1 Section 4.3:

- **Archetype A — Regulators, Standards-Setters, and Government Gatekeepers (7 entities):** Statutory authorities governing insurance supervision, deposit insurance, actuarial/financial-reporting standards, virtual assets, payment settlement, digital identity, and data privacy.
- **Archetype B — Insurance Underwriters, Reinsurers, Microinsurers, and Takaful Operators (22 entities):** Risk-bearing institutions licensed by NAICOM across general, life, composite, agricultural, microinsurance, Takaful, and reinsurance classes.
- **Archetype C — Insurtechs, Intermediaries, and Professional / Standard-Setting Bodies (16 entities):** Technology implementers, claims/distribution platforms, statutory broker/adjuster councils, and actuarial/insurance institutes.
- **Archetype D — Extended Ecosystem, Payments, Oracles, Identity, and Beneficiary-Side Organisations (18 entities):** Agricultural and MSME beneficiary groups (Northern and Southern sites), agro-meteorological agencies, instant-payment and PSB operators, SEC-incubated VASPs, NIMC verification providers, comparative African benchmarks, and Cardano technical teams.

### 1.2 Locked Access Status Vocabulary and Factual Reporting Baseline (Milestone 1 Section 4.4)

Every institution is classified using the exact access-status taxonomy locked in **Milestone 1 Section 4.4**, reflecting verified ground truth at the close of Milestone 2:

1. **`Warm — Prior/Symposium Contact (Invitation Not Sent)`:** Direct or institutional contact was established prior to or during *The Actuarial and Insurance Symposium 2.0* (26 August 2026) and the counterparty has orally expressed willingness to engage. In accordance with Milestone 1 Section 4.4 and Bias Control 9, **warm access is treated strictly as an access route, never as evidence of demand**. Informal relationship maintenance is ongoing; **formal written Milestone 3 research invitations, information sheets, and consent forms have not yet been sent** and no primary research interview has yet been conducted.
2. **`Open Communications — Invitation Not Sent`:** Preliminary institutional correspondence is under way (as recorded in Milestone 1 Section 4.3 for NEM Insurance and Linkage Assurance); formal written research invitations and interview scheduling will be executed in Milestone 3.
3. **`Referred — Alumni/Industry Route (Invitation Not Sent)`:** An established industry association, Symposium counterparty, or ASISA ABU alumni contact provides a direct referral route for Wave 3/4 outreach in Milestone 3.
4. **`Desk-Identified — No Contact Made`:** Identified and verified through public statutory registries, NAICOM post-recapitalisation lists, or technical documentation during Milestone 2 desk research; queued for Milestone 3 outreach or public technical specification evaluation.
5. **`Confirmed`:** *(Reserved strictly for Phase 4 / Milestone 3 per Milestone 1 Section 4.4: "Interview scheduled, consent obtained and screener criteria (Appendix B) met. Assigned only in Phase 4." Zero entities are marked `Confirmed` in Milestone 2).*

> **Sponsor-Affiliation Flag (Milestone 1 Section 6.4):** Institutions that sponsored or received recognition at *The Actuarial and Insurance Symposium 2.0* are explicitly tagged **`[Sponsor-Affiliated — M1 §6.4]`** in the tables below so that any claims they make during Milestone 3 interviews are independently corroborated under Bias Controls 5, 7, and 9.
