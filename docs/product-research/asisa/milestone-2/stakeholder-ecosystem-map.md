## 1. Purpose, Structure and Access Ground Truth

This register maps the institutions relevant to Phases 2 and 3 of the study under Milestone 1 Chapter 4 (§§4.1 to 4.8), Decision Gate 3 (§5.6) and §9.6. Starting from the 14 inception-stage organisations recorded in Milestone 1 §4.3, it catalogues **63 named organisations and beneficiary clusters** across Nigerian regulation, underwriting, reinsurance, microinsurance, Takaful, insurtech, brokerage, agriculture, identity, payments, virtual assets, comparative African supervision and the Cardano technical stack.

Mapping an organisation here does not imply that it wants or needs blockchain infrastructure. Several entries run the centralised systems against which the eight candidate use cases are tested, including the Nigerian Insurers Association's policy database (NIID), NAICOM's regulatory portal, NIBSS Instant Payments and commercial NIN verification APIs. Their presence in the register exists so Milestone 3 interviews can test whether any gap remains after those existing systems are accounted for.

### 1.1 Four-Archetype Taxonomy (Milestone 1 §4.3)

The register uses the four archetypes fixed in Milestone 1 §4.3 so that Bias Control 1 (Cardano ecosystem views weighted separately), Bias Control 3 (Northern and Southern sampling), Bias Control 5 (event-sample tagging) and Bias Control 7 (corroboration of sponsor claims) can be applied consistently during Milestone 3:

- **Archetype A: Regulators, standards-setters and government authorities (7 entries, `A01` to `A07`).** Statutory bodies overseeing insurance prudential and market-conduct rules, deposit protection, actuarial and accounting standards, securities and virtual assets, payment systems, national identity and data protection.
- **Archetype B: Underwriters, reinsurers, microinsurers and Takaful operators (22 entries, `B01` to `B22`).** Risk-bearing firms licensed by NAICOM across general, life, composite, agricultural, microinsurance, Takaful and reinsurance classes.
- **Archetype C: Insurtechs, intermediaries, professional bodies and academic advisors (16 entries, `C01` to `C16`).** Technology vendors, claims and embedded-distribution platforms, trade and statutory councils for underwriters, brokers and loss adjusters, actuarial bodies, Shariah scholars and academic reviewers.
- **Archetype D: Extended ecosystem, payments, identity, agriculture, beneficiary groups and Cardano actors (18 entries, `D01` to `D18`).** Smallholder and informal-sector associations in Northern and Southern Nigeria, agricultural risk and weather agencies, NIN verification providers, payment and stablecoin operators, comparative East African pilot actors, Cardano middleware teams and peer African regulatory references.

### 1.2 Access Status Vocabulary (Milestone 1 §4.4)

Each entry carries one of the access labels locked in Milestone 1 §4.4 as at the close of Milestone 2. No interviews have been conducted.

1. **`Warm: Prior/Symposium Contact (Invitation Not Sent)` (14 entries).** Institutional or individual contact was established before or during *The Actuarial and Insurance Symposium 2.0* (26 August 2026), with oral willingness to speak during the study. Under Milestone 1 §4.4 and Bias Control 9, warm access is an interview route only. It is never treated as evidence of product demand. Formal Milestone 3 research invitations, participant information sheets and consent forms have not yet been sent.
2. **`Open Communications: Invitation Not Sent` (2 entries).** Preliminary correspondence on scheduling is in place (recorded in Milestone 1 §4.3 for NEM Insurance and Linkage Assurance). Formal research invitations and screener checks await Milestone 3.
3. **`Referred: Alumni/Industry Route (Invitation Not Sent)` (19 entries).** Operationalises the supplementary alumni and institutional referral routes set out in Milestone 1 §§4.3 and 4.6 (`Wave 3` and `Wave 4`): a professional body, Symposium participant or ASISA ABU alumnus in the sector provides a named introduction route for Milestone 3 outreach, prior to any direct correspondence.
4. **`Desk-Identified: No Contact Made` (28 entries).** Identified from statutory registers, NAICOM's post-recapitalisation list, regulator notices or technical documentation during Milestone 2. No outreach has occurred.
5. **`Confirmed` (0 entries in Milestone 2).** Reserved under Milestone 1 §4.4 for Phase 4 (Milestone 3) once an interview is scheduled, informed consent is signed and Appendix B screener criteria are met.

### 1.3 Bias and Independence Flags (Milestone 1 §§6.4 and 7.8)

Two explicit flags govern how interview evidence from this register will be weighted in Milestones 3 and 4:

- **`[Sponsor-Affiliated: M1 §6.4]` and Symposium event tags.** Seven commercial firms sponsored the August 2026 Symposium (`B01` to `B06` and `C01`), two professional bodies contributed institutional marks (`C02`, `C04`), two public agencies attended as Guests of Honour (`A02`, `A03`), two institutional contacts participated without sponsorship (`A01`, `C03`), and academic presenters joined the review pool (`C16`). Under Bias Controls 5 and 7, statements from sponsor-affiliated firms cannot carry a viability rating unless corroborated by non-sponsor counterparties.
- **`[Cardano Ecosystem: Excluded from Criteria 1 and 2]` (`D13` to `D17`).** Under Bias Control 1, Cardano technical teams may be consulted solely on protocol parameters, middleware limits, integration effort and operator maintenance status (Criteria 3, 5 and 7). Their views are excluded from scoring Nigerian problem severity (Criterion 1) or market demand (Criterion 2).

**Contractual floor and counting rules (Milestone 1 §4.2).** Although the individual stratum minimums (`11 + 5 + 5 + 2`) sum to 23, the binding grant requirement for Milestone 3 is **not fewer than 25 completed interviews across Northern and Southern Nigeria** within a planned range of 25 to 35, including at least **11 Core interviews** as the Minimum Viable Evidence Base. At least two additional interviews above the 23 stratum minimums are drawn from the reserve pool across Archetypes A to D, and any declined invitation is replaced by same-archetype substitution from the 63-entry register (or under Bias Control 6 where a Desk-Identified regulator declines) so that completed interviews do not fall below 25. Only formal, consented, semi-structured interviews using the Milestone 1 Appendix A guides count toward the 25-interview floor, each documented with: (i) informed consent, (ii) a unique anonymised respondent ID, (iii) structured interview notes or transcript mapped to the research questions, and (iv) geographic and archetype classification, with any joint session involving multiple officials from the same organisational unit counted as one interview. Symposium panels, informal networking and scheduling calls do not count.
