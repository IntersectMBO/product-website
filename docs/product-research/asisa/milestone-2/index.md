## Summary

Milestone 2 covers Phases 2 and 3 of the Milestone 1 Research Design: market framing and literature review, preliminary regulatory scanning, stakeholder ecosystem mapping, the Appendix D infrastructure prerequisite check, and the Gate A pre-fieldwork provisional screen. Every output in this submission rests on secondary and tertiary sources only. No primary interviews or field visits have taken place.

Four conclusions define the desk baseline entering Milestone 3:

- **Every candidate use case faces an operating non-blockchain system in Nigeria.** The Nigerian Insurers Association already runs a central policy database (`NIID` / `askNIID` / `*565*11#` USSD), NAICOM runs an online regulatory portal for reinsurance placements, `NAIC`, `NIRSAL` and `Pula` administer agricultural index programmes off-chain, `Curacel` and `Octamile` automate Web2 claims workflows, pension fund administrators handle formal group life under PenCom's June 2025 fast-track rule, NIMC-licensed providers verify NIN and BVN in seconds under the NIMC Act 2026 National PKI framework, and NIBSS Instant Payments settled roughly 11 billion naira transfers in 2024. Under Milestone 1 §5.4, each candidate is presumed unviable unless Milestone 3 fieldwork proves a multi-party gap that expanding those centralised systems cannot solve more cheaply.
- **Zero cells reach `Strong` at Gate A.** Across 72 cells (`8` use cases by `9` locked criteria), the provisional pre-interview tally is **`0 Strong · 30 Conditional · 42 Weak (26 W + 16 W*) · 0 Disqualifying`**, with **`Low` desk-only confidence** on every use case. Under Milestone 1 §3.4, all eight candidates remain in the Milestone 3 interview guides (`40% / 40% / 20%` depth bands) because none hits an absolute primary-statute ban, and all Gate A estimates are archived before Phase 6 scoring.
- **2025 and 2026 Nigerian statutes restrict permissible ledger designs to back-office records.** NAICOM's *Guidelines for Insurtech Operations in Nigeria* (effective 1 August 2025) bar crypto premiums or claims without prior Commission approval (§12.1(vi)), bar unsupervised automated claim rejections (§12.1(iv); reinforced by NDPA 2023 s. 37), and require instant credit to a licensed insurer's bank account (§14.4; NIIRA 2025 s. 60(1)). NDPA 2023 erasure rules (s. 34(1)(d)) keep personal data off-chain.
- **Cardano has concrete middleware and operator gaps in Nigeria.** Regulated `cNGN` is live on eight non-Cardano networks and absent from Cardano (`RFP 03`), no Nigerian weather or yield oracle exists on Cardano (`RFP 07`), and `Orcfax` announced on 27 March 2026 a halt to research, development and platform enhancement with a contingency wind-down date of 31 July 2026. All seven project risks (`R01` to `R07`) and all ten Milestone 1 bias controls (`BC1` to `BC10`) remain **`Open: Mitigation Planned (Pending M3 Execution)`**.

## Challenges and Learnings

1. **Statutory turnover across 2025 and 2026.** Between March 2025 and August 2026 Nigeria enacted the *Investments and Securities Act 2025*, the *Nigerian Insurance Industry Reform Act 2025*, the *NAICOM Guidelines for Insurtech Operations in Nigeria* (1 August 2025) and the *NIMC Act 2026* (26 June 2026, setting out NIMC's National Public Key Infrastructure role), issued PenCom's 1 June 2025 circular removing prior approval on death-benefit processing, and completed insurance recapitalisation (2 August 2026). Secondary articles often cited repealed sections of the *Insurance Act 2003* or inaccurate composite capital figures. Checking primary statutes and regulator notices directly corrected those errors.
2. **Strength of existing centralised Nigerian systems.** Mapping each use case against its live Nigerian baseline showed that central databases and instant fiat rails already cover much of what generic blockchain insurance papers claim to solve (`NIA NIID` for policy verification, `NAICOM`'s portal and `SAP`/`SICS` for reinsurance filings, `Curacel` and `NIBSS NIP` for fast naira claims, and `NIMC` VSP APIs for two-second identity checks). That finding shifted Milestone 3's interview focus onto the narrower competitive-confidentiality, regulatory-audit and micro-unit-cost conditions under which a distributed record could add anything at all.
3. **Cardano middleware and operator fragility.** Beyond the absence of `cNGN` and Nigerian weather feeds on Cardano, `Orcfax`'s 27 March 2026 notice halting research and development (with a 31 July 2026 contingency wind-down date) illustrates the risk of relying on grant-funded middleware operators for regulated insurance products. Gate A incorporates that constraint directly into `UC1`, `UC4` and `UC5` infrastructure and delivery ratings.
