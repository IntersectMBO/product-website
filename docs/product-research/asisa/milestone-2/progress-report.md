## 4. Project Risk Register and Proposed Design Controls (To Be Tested in Milestone 3)

Because primary stakeholder consultations have not yet taken place, none of the project risks identified in Milestone 2 are treated as closed or mitigated at the desk stage. Instead, Table 4.1 records each risk as **Open** alongside the **planned architectural or methodological fix (`Proposed Design Control` / `Planned Fieldwork Control`)** that will be tested during Milestone 3 fieldwork.

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Current Risk Status & Severity | Proposed Design Control / Planned Fix (To Be Tested in Milestone 3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R01** | **Regulatory prohibition of crypto-denominated insurance flows in Nigeria** | **Open (High / Moderate)** | **Proposed Design Control (Fiat-Abstracted Architecture — Unverified in Fieldwork):** Direct inspection of *NAICOM Insurtech Guidelines 2025* **§12.1(vi)** shows that crypto premiums/claims require prior NAICOM approval, while **§14.4** requires instant credit to the insurer's bank account. The proposed fix separates B2B/fiat-settled architectures (`UC1`–`UC7`, using NIBSS NIP / PSB fiat settlement with zero crypto handling by end users) from token-settled cross-border/diaspora flows (`UC8`). Milestone 3 interviews with NAICOM (`A01`) and underwriters will test whether regulators accept this architectural separation. |
| **R02** | **Prohibition on unsupervised automated claims denials** | **Open (Moderate)** | **Proposed Design Control (Human-in-the-Loop Escalation Rule — Unverified in Fieldwork):** Because *NAICOM Insurtech Guidelines* **§12.1(iv)** and *NDPA 2023* **s. 37** prohibit solely automated claim rejections, the proposed fix for `UC5` restricts smart contracts to post-admission approval triggers and 60-day SLA compliance logging (**NIIRA s. 210**), while routing all potential denials to a human claims adjuster. Milestone 3 interviews will test regulator and underwriter acceptance of this asymmetrical rule. |
| **R03** | **Conflict between public blockchain immutability and NDPA 2023 right to erasure (s. 34(1)(d))** | **Open (Moderate)** | **Proposed Design Control (Off-Chain PII + Salted Hash / ZK Architecture — Unverified in Fieldwork):** The proposed fix across all eight candidate use cases keeps raw PII in off-chain domestic encrypted databases (retained for 10 years per **§17.2(ii)**), anchoring only salted `Blake2b-256` hashes, Merkle roots, **Hyperledger Identus** DIDs, or **Midnight** ZK proofs on Cardano. This proposed control will be tested with the NDPC (`A03`), NIMC (`A02`), and insurer DPOs in Milestone 3. |
| **R04** | **Absence of native Naira stablecoin (`cNGN`) and Nigerian weather feeds on Cardano** | **Open (High Infrastructure Gap)** | **Planned Fieldwork Probe & Cross-RFP Handoff:** Desk research confirmed that `cNGN` is not deployed on Cardano (`RFP 03` handoff) and that **Charli3 / Orcfax** do not carry NiMet/CHIRPS Nigerian weather feeds (`RFP 07` handoff). Milestone 3 Wave 3 consultations with NiMet, Charli3/Orcfax, WrappedCBDC (`cNGN`), and Busha/Quidax will test whether custom oracle adapters and edge fiat-conversion rails are technically and commercially feasible. |
| **R05** | **Sponsor-affiliation or event-sample bias in primary interviews** | **Open (Moderate Fieldwork Risk)** | **Planned Methodological Fix (Explicit Tagging & Non-Symposium Quota Enforcement):** All 12 Symposium sponsors/partners on the access roster are tagged `[Sponsor-Affiliated: M1 §6.4]` in the Stakeholder Ecosystem Map, and the Milestone 3 recruitment plan allocates Waves 2–5 across 49 non-symposium institutions/groups in Northern and Southern Nigeria (enforcing Bias Controls 3, 5, 7, and 9 during primary data collection). |
