## 1. Summary of Milestone 2 Execution

Milestone 2 covers Phases 2 and 3 of the Milestone 1 Research Design: market framing and literature review, preliminary regulatory scanning across eight candidate use cases, stakeholder ecosystem mapping, the Appendix D infrastructure prerequisite check, and the Gate A pre-fieldwork provisional screen. All deliverables rest on secondary and tertiary sources only. Zero primary research interviews or field visits were conducted during Milestone 2, preserving the boundary before Milestone 3 primary data collection.

## 4. Project Risk, Limitation and Bias Control Register

Because primary data collection has not started, no operational, regulatory or methodological risk that depends on Milestone 3 execution is marked closed or mitigated. Every item below carries an open or planned status together with the specific verification step required in Milestone 3.

### 4.1 Project Risks and Desk Limitations

**Table 4.1: Risk register at the close of Milestone 2**

| ID | Risk or limitation | Status at Milestone 2 close | Planned control or design hypothesis | How Milestone 3 will verify it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R01** | **Restriction on crypto-denominated premiums and claims, plus instant bank-credit rules** (`NAICOM Insurtech Guidelines §12.1(vi)`, `§14.4`; `NIIRA 2025 s. 60(1)`) | **Open: Mitigation Planned (Pending M3 Execution)** | Test back-office ledger designs where premiums and claims settle in naira over `NIBSS NIP` or `PSBs` straight to the licensed insurer's bank account (`UC1` to `UC7`), separating them from `UC8` | Module 1 interviews with `NAICOM` (`A01`) and `NDIC` (`A02`) testing whether naira-settled back-office records avoid `§12.1(vi)` and satisfy `§14.4` |
| **R02** | **Prohibition on unsupervised automated claim rejections** (`Insurtech Guidelines §12.1(iv)`; `NDPA 2023 s. 37`) | **Open: Mitigation Planned (Pending M3 Execution)** | Confine `UC5` hypothesis to post-admission payout triggers and timestamping of notification, admission and human escalation under `NIIRA s. 210` | Module 1, 2 and 4 interviews with `NAICOM` (`A01`), `ILAN` (`C07`) and underwriters checking regulator acceptance and value over internal logs |
| **R03** | **Conflict between public ledger immutability, erasure rights and cross-border data rules** (`NDPA 2023 ss. 24, 30, 34(1)(d), 41 to 43`) | **Open: Mitigation Planned (Pending M3 Execution)** | Keep all personal, medical and farm-location data off-chain in domestic systems (`§17.2(ii)`); test salted hashes, `Identus`/`Veridian` keys or `Midnight` proofs on-chain | Wave 3 outreach to `NDPC` (`A07`), `NIMC` (`A06`), licensed data-protection practitioners and insurer data protection officers on whether foreign validator replication of salted hashes triggers `ss. 41 to 43` |
| **R04** | **Cardano infrastructure and operator gaps (`cNGN` absence, missing Nigerian weather feeds and `Orcfax` R&D halt)** | **Open: Mitigation Planned (Pending M3 Execution)** | Treat `cNGN` absence (`RFP 03`) and missing weather feeds (`RFP 07`) as active `Weak` constraints in Gate A; remove `Orcfax` (`D14`, halted R&D on 27 March 2026 with 31 July 2026 contingency wind-down) as an assumed builder | Wave 3 calls with `WrappedCBDC` (`D09`), `Busha`/`Quidax` (`D10`), `NiMet` (`D04`) and `Charli3` (`D13`) to establish realistic custom build cost or confirm `No-Go` |
| **R05** | **Symposium sponsor-affiliation and event-sample bias** across the `14` Symposium-connected `Warm` entries (`7` commercial sponsors `B01` to `B06` and `C01`, `2` partner-mark bodies `C02` and `C04`, `2` Guest-of-Honour agencies `A02` and `A03`, `2` non-sponsor Symposium contacts `A01` and `C03`, and `1` academic/advisor pool `C16`) | **Open: Mitigation Planned (Pending M3 Execution)** | Tag all Symposium-linked entries in the [Stakeholder Ecosystem Map](./stakeholder-ecosystem-map.md) and draw Waves 2 to 5 across the `49` non-symposium organisations and beneficiary clusters in Northern and Southern Nigeria | Audit the Milestone 3 consultation log to ensure non-sponsor corroboration for every finding (Bias Controls 5 and 7) and enforce Bias Control 9 adoption-signal thresholds |
| **R06** | **Redundancy against operating Nigerian centralised systems** (`NIA NIID`, `NAICOM` portal, `NAIC`/`NIRSAL`/`Pula`, `Curacel`/`Octamile`, `PenCom` PFAs, `NIMC` VSPs, `NIBSS NIP`) | **Open: Mitigation Planned (Pending M3 Execution)** | Score `0 Strong` at Gate A and structure Part A of every interview around how current centralised systems work and fail before mentioning blockchain | Administer Part A (`[A]`) questions first in all `≥25` interviews; recommend `No-Go` in Milestone 4 wherever central database upgrades prove cheaper and adequate |
| **R07** | **Non-response risk among `Desk-Identified` Wave 3 regulators and operators** (`SEC A04`, `CBN A05`, `NIMC A06`, `NDPC A07` and `24` other Desk entries) | **Open: Mitigation Planned (Pending M3 Execution)** | Use institutional referrals (`NIA`, `CIIN`, `NAICOM`) for formal Wave 3 outreach and apply Milestone 1 Bias Control 6 substitution rules if a regulator declines | Log all outreach attempts; where a former official or licensed intermediary (`VSP`, `ARIP VASP`, compliance counsel) is substituted under Bias Control 6, downgrade and disclose the confidence rating in Milestone 3 and Milestone 4 |
