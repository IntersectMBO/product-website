## Summary

Milestone 1 establishes the design infrastructure for the research project before any primary research is committed. The core objective is alignment: ensuring the Cardano Product Committee (CPC) and the ASISA research team agree on decision gates, use-case hypotheses, screening criteria, interview methodology, bias controls, and ethical protocols before fieldwork begins.

During this milestone, the research team:

- **Conducted the project kickoff** (August 2026), confirming scope, decision gates, and communication cadence.
- **Hosted the ASISA Insurance Technology Symposium** (26 August 2026), securing direct industry relationships with 12+ tier-one stakeholders including NAICOM, CIIN, Leadway Assurance, Heirs Insurance, NSIA, NDIC, and others.
- **Produced a comprehensive Research Design Document** covering the 8-phase methodology, 8 candidate use cases entering as hypotheses, 9-criterion screening model, structured interview guides, respondent screener, 10 bias controls, decision gate mapping, and an integrated ethics brief.
- **Developed a Stakeholder Recruitment Plan** de-risked by confirmed warm access to regulators, underwriters, insurtechs, Takaful operators, and professional bodies.
- **Established the cadence and checkpoint schedule** across the 24-week contractual arc, including the 14-week post-approval execution schedule completing in January 2027.

No primary research findings are presented at this stage. All use cases enter the screening model as hypotheses, and the study is designed to produce a defensible no-go finding if the evidence does not support entry.

## Challenges and Learnings

1. **Symposium scheduling overlap:** The ASISA Insurance Technology Symposium (26 August 2026) was held during the initial project period, which shifted the formal documentation timeline. However, the symposium provided significant strategic value by establishing direct industry relationships with 12+ insurance sector stakeholders.

2. **Stakeholder access stronger than projected:** The proposal categorised several counterparties as "desk-identified" or "referred." Following the symposium, multiple stakeholders including NAICOM, CIIN, NDIC, and several major insurers have been upgraded to "warm" or "confirmed" access.
