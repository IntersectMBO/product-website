# Sampling Framework - Section 6 of [Complete Research Design Document](./Complete%20Research%20Design%20Document.md)

The sampling framework applies the same rules to all three hubs.

## Quantitative Survey 

The target is a minimum of ninety completed surveys per country for a combined total of at least two hundred and seventy across Nigeria, Kenya and Ghana. Respondents are recruited through hub coordinator networks, university partnerships, fintech and developer community groups and local enterprise contacts. Every respondent must pass the screener in Part A. Anyone who answers Yes to being an active paid/unpaid contributor to the Cardano ecosystem is excluded from the main sample and analysed separately as described in Section 2 of [Complete Research Design Document](./Complete%20Research%20Design%20Document.md) 

## Qualitative Interviews 

Thirty interviews in total, ten per hub, split as shown in the table below. This matches the split described in the original Proposal with external developers at nine in total, enterprise and corporate buyers at twelve in total and capital allocators and analysts at nine in total.

| Hub | Survey Target | Interviews (Developers) | Interviews (Enterprise/Fintech) | Interviews (Analysts/Capital) |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Nigeria | 90 | 3 | 4 | 3 |
| Kenya | 90 | 3 | 4 | 3 |
| Ghana | 90 | 3 | 4 | 3 |
| Total | 270 | 9 | 12 | 9 |

## Recruitment Quality Control 

Before an interview is logged as valid, each hub lead confirms that the respondent meets the role definition for their category and is not a current paid Cardano contributor. A short recruitment log is kept for every respondent, recording how they were found and confirming they meet the screening criteria. This creates a paper trail that Intersect can check if requested. Excluded insider respondents are recorded in the same way, with their own log, so the count of who was screened out and why is auditable alongside the primary sample.

## Limitations 

This sample is not a random probability sample of the general population and is not intended to be. It follows a purposive criterion based sampling logic, thus selecting respondents whose professional vantage point is directly relevant to Cardano's adoption decisions in these three markets (Pahwa et al., 2023). Findings should therefore be read as directional, criterion sampled evidence and not as a nationally representative statistic.
