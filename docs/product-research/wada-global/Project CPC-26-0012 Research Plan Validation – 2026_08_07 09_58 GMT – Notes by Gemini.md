### **Summary**

The research project team finalized methodology and sampling targets for the Cardano brand perception study in Africa.

**Establishing research methodology standards**  
The project utilizes a convergent mixed-methods approach with a sample of 300 across 3 hubs. Survey instruments capture data from non-Cardano users to identify adoption barriers.

**Solving participant recruitment challenges**  
The team authorized the use of snowballing techniques to supplement standard recruitment for enterprise stakeholders. Recruitment difficulties will be captured as research limitations rather than reducing project targets.

**Refining research participant categories**  
Surveys will capture broad data with user status serving as a secondary classification. Interviews prioritize individuals not currently using the Cardano ecosystem to understand alternative platform preferences.

### **Decisions**

## Aligned

* **Retention of established sampling distribution** The team committed to maintaining the original sampling distribution for qualitative interviews, choosing to categorize potential recruitment difficulties as a research limitation rather than reducing the target quota for enterprise participants.

* **Documentation structure for methodology clarity** The project documentation will be updated to relocate the sampling table within the methodological approach section to ensure alignment with explanatory text and improve clarity regarding the target population.

* **Survey screening and data classification defined** The survey screening process will rely on respondent self-identification, and all collected data will be analyzed regardless of user status, with respondents categorized by their exposure to the Cardano ecosystem for analysis.

* **Interview focus set on non-users** The interview process will prioritize individuals who are not currently using the Cardano ecosystem to facilitate a comparison with other blockchains.

### **Details**

* **Meeting Purpose and Scope**: Samuel Kobi convened the team to review Milestone One of the research project, which focuses on brand perception and positioning for Cardano across three specific hubs: Lagos, Nairobi, and Accra. The goal of the meeting was to establish a standardized research methodology and a data-driven baseline before the commencement of fieldwork ([00:10:26](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.28qzn7y0kzds)).

* **Research Methodology**: The project will utilize a convergent mixed-methods approach, which combines quantitative surveys with in-depth qualitative interviews. This methodology is designed to measure brand awareness and consideration, specifically focusing on how factors like trust and usability influence adoption by developers and enterprises ([00:20:49](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.vuan44afho1d)).

* **Quantitative Survey Structure**: The survey instrument is structured to exclude existing Cardano users through a non-contaminated screening process to ensure unbiased results. The survey includes sections to measure unprompted brand familiarity and includes direct comparison questions against competitors, such as Solana and Ethereum Layer 2 solutions, to identify specific adoption barriers ([00:24:17](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.b7p60kjm0yc1)).

* **Qualitative Interview Design**: The team will conduct 45-minute, semi-structured interviews to obtain contextual data regarding personal experiences and friction points. These interviews are intended to explore specific proof points, such as tooling upgrades and security audits, which are critical for influencing decision-makers, including developers and institutional leaders ([00:25:48](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.t4w6ttnfmvs2)).

* **Sampling Targets and Distribution**: The research aims for a total sample of 300, comprising 270 quantitative survey respondents (90 per hub) and 30 key informant interviews (10 per hub). The qualitative interview breakdown for each hub is designed to include three developers, four enterprise or fintech experts, and three capital allocators ([00:27:04](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.dg8322f9nl2j)) ([00:41:49](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.7r9tlecdov2m)).

* **Quality Control and Participant Recruitment**: Quality control is considered paramount for the project's success. The team will strictly screen out active, paid Cardano ecosystem contributors to prevent bias and is required to maintain full recruitment audit logs. The recruitment strategy utilizes purposive criterion-based sampling aimed at industry decision-makers ([00:28:37](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.azejmr5qocdh)).

* **Enterprise Participant Recruitment Challenges**: Abdulrahman Abdulbasit Adigun expressed concerns regarding the difficulty of securing interviews with enterprise and fintech stakeholders in Nigeria due to a lack of strong, established relationships. They suggested that if accessing these participants remains difficult, the team should acknowledge this as a research limitation rather than altering the core methodology ([00:36:21](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.r8tr3vybmm7i)) ([00:38:37](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.mwvmrxpuiuwk)).

* **Proposed Sampling Techniques**: Nathaniel Dwamena proposed supplementing the purposive sampling method with a snowballing technique, which would allow participants to refer other relevant individuals for interviews. They also suggested that if individual interviews remain difficult to secure, the teams could organize focus group sessions to facilitate group discussions among potential participants ([00:47:10](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.a1ei74tgfbqn)).

* **Strategies for Engaging Hesitant Participants**: To mitigate concerns regarding confidentiality, Nathaniel Dwamena emphasized the need to reassure potential participants that their information will remain anonymous and confidential. By explicitly communicating that the research will not expose business vulnerabilities, the team hopes to increase the willingness of fintech and enterprise leaders to participate in the study ([00:52:11](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.r4ervudvwak6)).

* **Management of Research Constraints**: Samuel Kobi advised that the team should proceed with the established project targets rather than preemptively reducing them. If the teams encounter difficulties on the ground, these obstacles should be documented as research limitations in the final report to inform future study iterations ([00:55:19](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.djcfgugxtk1v)).

* **Terminology and Documentation Alignment**: Nathaniel Dwamena noted confusion regarding the terminology used in the documentation, specifically the distinction between "survey target" and the total research population ([00:57:33](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.6jcsc692g70h)) ([01:00:34](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.wx19x7fmts89)). It was agreed that the documentation requires clearer labeling to distinguish between total survey targets and non-Cardano user requirements ([01:02:19](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.li7msuiagwp3)). Nana Safo agreed to update the methodology section to ensure alignment and clarity for all stakeholders ([01:03:04](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.tje470nhoikd)).

* **Competitive Comparison Logic**: Abdulrahman Abdulbasit Adigun questioned how respondents could compare their experiences with other blockchains against Cardano if they are excluded from the Cardano ecosystem ([01:04:51](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.kike81l954pd)). Nana Safo and Samuel Kobi clarified that excluding Cardano users does not prevent respondents from having informed perspectives on Cardano or comparing it to other ecosystems. The methodology allows for valid market intelligence by capturing perceptions from those external to the specific Cardano user base ([01:05:56](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.owqhfx75e7zu)) ([01:08:05](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.sqequmijn4c0)).

* **Defining Research Participant Categories**: The team discussed how to distinguish between Cardano users and non-Cardano users during the survey process. Herine Omollo raised a concern regarding how to categorize individuals who were previously involved in the Cardano ecosystem but have since ceased participation. Samuel Kobi clarified that the research would rely on the respondents themselves to self-identify as either Cardano or non-Cardano users via a simple "yes" or "no" question within the survey ([01:09:15](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.c0jmja4rl57w)).

* **Data Analysis Methodology**: Nana Safo explained that the data analysis strategy will utilize responses from both Cardano and non-Cardano users to gain comprehensive insights. Even if a participant is screened as a current or former Cardano user, their data will be analyzed to understand the motivations for their participation or their rationale for opting out of the ecosystem, which is essential for making informed judgments ([01:10:21](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.s5nwby2jijic)).

* **Strategy for Interviews versus Surveys**: The team clarified the operational approach for gathering feedback from different groups. Herine Omollo inquired whether interviewers should exclusively target non-Cardano users. Nana Safo advised that interviews should prioritize individuals who are not currently using the Cardano ecosystem—including those who may have some exposure to it but prefer other blockchains—to understand why they have chosen alternatives. In contrast, the online survey will be distributed broadly, and a screening question will be used after the survey is completed to classify the respondents' status ([01:12:34](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.ya9f8dkqnebj)).

* **Clarification of Research Objectives**: Nathaniel Dwamena sought to verify if the research was restricted to former Cardano users who migrated to other ecosystems. Nana Safo and Samuel Kobi clarified that while former users provide valuable insights into their rationale for leaving, the primary goal of the research is to understand general perceptions of the Cardano ecosystem and why users may prefer other platforms. The team agreed that the survey will capture broad data, with user status serving as a secondary classification, while interviews will specifically target developers, investors, and enterprise-oriented individuals ([01:13:46](?tab=t.mtnve5by219v#heading=h.fytxohj9v8xj)).
