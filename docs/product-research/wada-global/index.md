# Wada Global

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **RFP** | RFP 04 — Brand Awareness & Perception |
| **Grant** | 22,500 ADA |

## Scope

Cost-efficient brand perception research across West African markets. Research assesses Cardano's awareness and perception among enterprise decision-makers, developers, and investors in the region, surfaces reputation gaps relative to competing chains, and delivers actionable recommendations for brand positioning.

Milestone reports submitted by Wada Global will appear here.

## Milestones

| Milestone | Services | Deliverables | Acceptance Criteria | Timeframe |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Milestone 1** – Research Design & Cross-Hub Alignment | Research design, methodology and cross-hub alignment across the three national hubs (Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana), including instrument design, sampling approach and confirmation of the message-testing framework. | Research design and methodology; cross-hub alignment plan; survey and interview instruments; sampling framework. | A complete research design document is delivered, clearly outlining research objectives, approach and rationale for chosen methods. Methodology must be appropriate for cross-country comparative research across Nigeria, Kenya and Ghana. All artefacts (survey, interview instruments) must be adequately documented, reviewed and aligned across all hubs. | **04/08/2026** |
| **Milestone 2** – Sponsored Hub Fieldwork & Primary Collection | Sponsored hub fieldwork and primary data collection, including surveys and 30 interviews across Nigeria, Kenya and Ghana. | Completed surveys; 30 interview records across the three hubs; primary data set; fieldwork progress log. | A total of 30 surveys are conducted across Nigeria, Kenya & Ghana with distribution aligned with the sampling plan. Each interview must have a complete record, including date, location, interviewer and category, and are fully administered. | **01/09/2026** |
| **Milestone 3** – Cross-Border Benchmarking & Data Synthesis | Cross-border benchmarking and data synthesis, consolidating the primary and secondary evidence into structured findings. | Cross-border benchmarking analysis; consolidated data synthesis; draft audience map and objection register. | A benchmarking framework is delivered that defines the criteria, indicators and comparison dimensions used to evaluate findings across all mentioned countries. The cross-border benchmark must be consistent with the research design and testing framework from Milestone 1. Consolidated data synthesis must resolve any discrepancies between datasets. | **15/09/2026** |
| **Milestone 4** – Final Output Production & CPS Authoring | Final output production and authoring of the Multi-Hub Cardano Problem Statement (CPS), plus community knowledge-sharing. | African Decision Memo; Three-Nation Audience Map; Regional Objection Register; Competitor Positioning Matrix; Multi-Hub Cardano Problem Statement (CPS). | A complete final output package is produced, including all benchmarked findings and three-nation audience map, competitor positioning matrix and CPS. Final outputs must be clear, coherent, evidence-based and aligned with the message-testing framework. | **30/09/2026** |
| **Total Timeframe from Effective Date** | | | | **10 weeks** |
