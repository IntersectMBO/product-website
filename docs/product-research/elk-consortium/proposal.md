## Executive Summary
Cardano's 2030 Vision targets $3B TVL, 27M+ monthly transactions, and 1M active wallets. Closing the gap between today's figures and those targets requires more than infrastructure improvements — it requires a structured, replicable model for how real-world vertical industries translate their adoption activity into measurable ecosystem-wide KPI progress.

ELK, a blockchain consulting firm with hands-on experience onboarding companies into the Cardano ecosystem, proposes to lead a two-part research initiative:

**Part 1 — Build the Framework.** ELK will design, validate, and publish a complete **Vertical-to-KPI Impact Framework**: a universal methodology that any Cardano vertical team can use to map their business activities to Cardano's 2030 North Star KPIs, model adoption scenarios, and identify critical bottlenecks and enablers.

**Part 2 — Apply the Framework.** The completed framework will then be applied to three of Cardano's most strategically relevant verticals — **Governance**, **Agricultural Supply Chain**, and **Bitcoin DeFi** — each led by a specialized domain partner, validating the framework's replicability and generating deep, actionable intelligence for each vertical.

This structure ensures the ecosystem receives two compounding outputs: a long-term community asset in the form of a reusable methodology, and three immediate vertical intelligence reports that directly inform the Product Committee's 2026 roadmap.

## Core Research Question
*"What is the right universal model for mapping any Cardano vertical's business activities to the ecosystem's 2030 KPIs — and does that model hold when applied to verticals as different as Governance, Agricultural Supply Chain, and Bitcoin DeFi?" *

## Problem Statement
The Cardano ecosystem has identified multiple high-priority verticals for growth, yet a persistent and critical gap remains: **there is no structured, replicable methodology connecting vertical execution activities to the Cardano 2030 KPIs. **

Without this connection:

●​ Vertical investments are evaluated without projected KPI impact​  ●​ Cross-vertical comparisons remain subjective and inconsistent​  ●​ The community and DReps lack the data infrastructure to assess whether vertical bets are paying off​  ●​ Each vertical team independently reinvents its own reporting model — with no shared language or benchmark​

This proposal addresses that gap at the root. Rather than producing a vertical report that happens to mention KPIs, it first builds the underlying model that makes rigorous vertical-to-KPI analysis possible — then stress-tests and validates that model across three fundamentally different verticals. The result is both a methodology that outlasts this grant and three vertical intelligence reports with immediate practical value.

## Research Objectives
**Primary Objective​ **Design, validate, and publish a complete Vertical-to-KPI Impact Framework that is replicable across any Cardano vertical — and apply it to three strategic verticals (Governance, Agri Supply Chain, Bitcoin DeFi).

**Secondary Objectives **

## PART 1 — The Vertical-to-KPI Impact Framework
*Lead: ELK | Duration: Months 1–2 | Budget: 35,000 ADA *

### What We Are Building
Part 1 produces a fully documented, community-validated **Vertical-to-KPI Impact Framework** — a universal research methodology that any Cardano vertical team can use to:

1.​ Map their vertical's business activities to Cardano's 2030 North Star KPIs​

 2.​ Model adoption scenarios and their projected KPI contributions​

 3.​ Identify the critical bottlenecks and enablers that determine impact at scale​

 4.​ Report progress in a format that maps directly to Cardano's 2030 Vision metrics​

The framework is designed to be self-replicating: by the end of Part 1, any prospective vertical lead should be able to pick up the methodology and apply it independently.

### Competitive Benchmark Methodology
Competitive benchmarking across all three verticals will follow a consistent evaluation framework applied by each domain partner. For each vertical, Cardano will be assessed against the two to four most relevant competing platforms or protocols across the following criteria: tooling maturity (availability and quality of developer and operator tooling), adoption depth (number of active operators, real deployments, and on-chain activity), TVL and liquidity (where applicable), regulatory positioning (how each platform navigates the regulatory environment of the vertical), and narrative credibility (how the platform is perceived by operators and institutions outside the crypto space).** **

### Framework Components
The Impact Framework is built around four core components:

**1. The Impact Vertical Chain Template​ ** A structured model tracing the journey from a single end-user or operator action to its aggregate contribution to Cardano's KPIs. For any vertical, the Impact Vertical chain defines:

●​ The key actors (end users, operators, institutions, dApps, bridges)​  ●​ The on-chain events each actor generates (identity, transactions, payments, votes, liquidity)​  ●​ The causal link between those events and each of Cardano's 7 North Star KPIs​

**2. The Scenario Modeling Engine​ ** A standardized approach to modeling vertical adoption at three scales — conservative, moderate, and optimistic — with a shared set of assumptions, input variables, and KPI output metrics. This ensures all three vertical applications are genuinely comparable.

**3. The Bottleneck & Enabler Taxonomy​ ** A classification system for the factors that accelerate or constrain vertical adoption on Cardano — covering UX/tooling gaps, liquidity availability, regulatory friction, BD capacity, and ecosystem infrastructure. Applied consistently across verticals, this taxonomy enables cross-vertical diagnosis.

**4. The Vertical Impact Scorecard​ ** A one-page planning and reporting tool derived from the framework, designed for vertical leads to self-assess their KPI trajectory, set evidence-based targets, and report progress to the community in a standardized format.

## PART 2 — Vertical Applications
*Duration: Months 2–4 | Budget: 75,000 ADA (25,000 ADA per vertical) *

With the framework completed and validated, Part 2 applies it in full to three strategic verticals — simultaneously proving the framework's replicability and delivering deep vertical intelligence across Governance, Agricultural Supply Chain, and Bitcoin DeFi.

Each vertical application follows the same structure: Impact Chain analysis → competitive benchmarking → scenario modeling → bottleneck identification → KPI projections. This structural consistency makes the three reports genuinely comparable and the findings aggregatable into the cross-vertical KPI matrix.

### What an Impact Vertical Chain Is
An Impact Vertical Chain is a step-by-step causal map answering one question: **if one real participant uses Cardano within this vertical, exactly what happens on-chain, and how does that contribute to Cardano's 2030 KPIs? **

Each row represents one concrete action taken by a real actor in the vertical. The table reads left to right: the **Stage** names where we are in the adoption journey, the **Activity** describes what specifically happens on-chain, and the **KPI Impact** column identifies which of Cardano's North Star metrics that activity moves — and in which direction.

The power of the model is in what happens when you scale it: a single row might describe one farmer, one governance entity, or one Bitcoin user. When that action is repeated by 1,000, then 10,000, then 100,000 participants, the aggregate KPI contribution becomes quantifiable. That is

exactly what the scenario modeling in Part 2 does — it takes each Impact Chain and runs it through three adoption scales to produce concrete KPI projections.

These Impact Chains presented in this proposal are **illustrative previews** — they show the structure and logic of the model. The full, validated, data-backed Impact Chains are the core deliverable of Part 2.

### Risk & Mitigation Plan
**Partner Delivery Risk**​  This research involves four independent teams working in parallel. To ensure consistency and on-time delivery, ELK will maintain a shared coordination rhythm across all partners throughout the project, with monthly check-ins and a shared delivery tracker. Each partner's scope and deliverables are clearly defined and contractually agreed prior to project start.

**Data Availability Risk**​  On-chain data for emerging verticals on Cardano may be limited or inconsistent in some areas. Where verified data is unavailable, assumptions underpinning scenario models will be clearly documented, sourced, and distinguished from data-backed projections.

**Interview Access Risk**​  Securing 15+ substantive operator interviews across three verticals within a five-month window carries scheduling risk. Outreach will begin in Month 1 — before the framework is finalized — to ensure the interview pipeline is active well ahead of the vertical application phase.

**Scope Creep Risk**​  The framework design phase (Part 1) could expand beyond its two-month window if validation cycles are not tightly managed. ELK will set a hard finalization date for the framework at the end of Month 2, with community feedback consolidated into a single iteration round rather than open-ended revision cycles.

## Problem Statement

The Cardano ecosystem has identified multiple high-priority verticals for growth, yet a persistent and critical gap remains: **there is no structured, replicable methodology connecting vertical execution activities to the Cardano 2030 KPIs.**

Without this connection:

- Vertical investments are evaluated without projected KPI impact
- Cross-vertical comparisons remain subjective and inconsistent
- The community and DReps lack the data infrastructure to assess whether vertical bets are paying off
- Each vertical team independently reinvents its own reporting model — with no shared language or benchmark

This proposal addresses that gap at the root. Rather than producing a vertical report that happens to mention KPIs, it builds the underlying model that makes rigorous vertical-to-KPI analysis possible — then stress-tests and validates that model across three fundamentally different verticals. The result is both a methodology that outlast this grant and three vertical intelligence reports with immediate practical value.

## PART 1 — The Vertical-to-KPI Impact Framework

Lead: ELK | Duration: Months 1–2 | Budget: 35,000 ADA

### What We Are Building

Part 1 produces a fully documented, community-validated **Vertical-to-KPI Impact Framework** — a universal research methodology that any Cardano vertical team can use to:


1. Map their vertical's business activities to Cardano's 2030 North Star KPIs
2. Model adoption scenarios and their projected KPI contributions
3. Identify the critical bottlenecks and enablers that determine impact at scale
4. Report progress in a format that maps directly to Cardano's 2030 Vision metrics

The framework is designed to be self-replicating: by the end of Part 1, any prospective vertical lead should be able to pick up the methodology and apply it independently.

## Competitive Benchmark Methodology

Competitive benchmarking across all three verticals will follow a consistent evaluation framework applied by each domain partner. For any vertical, Cardano will be assessed against the two to four most relevant competing platforms or protocols across the following criteria: tooling maturity (availability and quality of developer and operator tooling), adoption depth (number of active operators, real deployments, and on-chain activity), TVL and liquidity (where applicable), regulatory positioning (how each platform navigates the regulatory environment of the vertical), and narrative credibility (how the platform is perceived by operators and institutions outside the crypto space).

## Framework Components

The Impact Framework is built around four core components:

### 1. The Impact Vertical Chain Template
A structured model tracing the journey from a single end-user or operator action to its aggregate contribution to Cardano's KPIs. For any vertical, the Impact vertical chain defines:

- The key actors (end users, operators, institutions, dApps, bridges)
- The on-chain events each actor generates (identity, transactions, payments, votes, liquidity)
- The causal link between those events and each of Cardano's 7 North Star KPIs

### 2. The Scenario Modeling Engine
A standardized approach to modeling vertical adoption at three scales — conservative, moderate, and optimistic — with a shared set of assumptions, input variables, and KPI output metrics. This ensures all three vertical applications are genuinely comparable.


## 3. The Bottleneck & Enabler Taxonomy
A classification system for the factors that accelerate or constrain vertical adoption on Cardano — covering UX/tooling gaps, liquidity availability, regulatory friction, BD capacity, and ecosystem infrastructure. Applied consistently across verticals, this taxonomy enables cross-vertical diagnosis.

## 4. The Vertical Impact Scorecard
A one-page planning and reporting tool derived from the framework, designed for vertical leads to self-assess their KPI trajectory, set evidence-based targets, and report progress to the community in a standardized format.

### Phase 1 — Analysis (Weeks 1–3)
- Review and synthesize all existing Cardano 2030 KPI definitions
- Audit existing vertical reporting approaches across Cardano ecosystem teams
- Benchmark methodologies from adjacent ecosystems (Polkadot, Ethereum ecosystem funds, Cosmos) to identify best practices
- Draft the initial Impact vertical template and scenario modeling structure
- Publish a public call for ecosystem input on Intersect forums

### Phase 2 — Framework Design & Expert Validation (Weeks 4–8)
- Conduct 10–12 structured interviews with vertical leads, dApp builders, BD professionals, and DREps across the Cardano ecosystem
- Iterate the framework based on expert input, stress-testing the Impact Vertical Chain template against multiple hypothetical verticals
- Validate the Bottleneck & Enabler Taxonomy with practitioners from at least 3 different ecosystem domains
- Host a live community workshop (Week 7) presenting the draft framework to ~30 community members for open feedback
- Finalize and publish the complete framework documentation on Intersect platform

This structural consistency makes the three reports genuinely comparable and the findings aggregatable into the cross-vertical KPI matrix.

## What an Impact Vertical Chain Is

An Impact Vertical Chain is a step-by-step causal map answering one question: **if one real participant uses Cardano within this vertical, exactly what happens on-chain, and how does that contribute to Cardano's 2030 KPIs?**

Each row represents one concrete action taken by a real actor in the vertical. The table reads left to right: the **Stage** names where we are in the adoption journey, the **Activity** describes what specifically happens on-chain, and the **KPI Impact** column identifies which of Cardano's North Star metrics that activity moves — and in which direction.

The power of the model is what happens when you scale it: a single row might describe one farmer, one governance entity, or one Bitcoin user. When that action is repeated by 1,000, then 10,000, then 100,000 participants, the aggregate KPI contribution becomes quantifiable. That is


exactly what the scenario modeling in Part 2 does — it takes each Impact Chain and runs it through three adoption scales to produce concrete KPI projections.

These Impact Chains presented in this proposal are **illustrative previews** — they show the structure and logic of the model. The full, validated, data-backed Impact Chains are the core deliverable of Part 2.

# Risk & Mitigation Plan

## Partner Delivery Risk

This research involves four independent teams working in parallel. To ensure consistency and on-time delivery, ELK will maintain a shared coordination rhythm across all partners throughout the project, with monthly check-ins and a shared delivery tracker. Each partner's scope and deliverables are clearly defined and contractually agreed prior to project start.

## Data Availability Risk

On-chain data for emerging verticals on Cardano can be limited or inconsistent in some areas. Where verified data is unavailable, assumptions underpinning scenario models will be clearly documented, sourced, and distinguished from data-backed projections.

## Interview Access Risk

Securing 15+ substantive operator interviews across three verticals within a five-month window carries scheduling risk. Outreach will begin in Month 1 — before the framework is finalized — to ensure the interview pipeline is active ahead of the vertical application phase.


## Scope Creep Risk
The framework design phase (Part 1) could expand beyond its two-month window if validation cycles are not tightly managed. ELK will set a hard finalization date for the framework at the end of Month 2, with community feedback consolidated into a single iteration round rather than open-ended revision cycles.
