## **Data-Source and External Stakeholder Map**

This map identifies the external stakeholders and intended sources of evidence that will inform the development and validation of the Vertical-to-KPI Impact Framework. Milestone 1 establishes the planned research inputs and engagement approach; detailed vertical analyses and scenario modelling will be completed in later milestones.

“Planned” indicates an intended research activity, not a completed analysis or confirmed data-access arrangement. For completed discussions, we will maintain a dated engagement record and concise notes, subject to participant consent and confidentiality requirements. Discussions will not be described as formal validation unless that role was explicitly undertaken.

**Interpretation and limitations.** On-chain transaction data can indicate observable network activity, but does not on its own establish unique users, real-world adoption, economic value, or causal contribution to Cardano’s KPIs. We will state the attribution method, coverage period, and material limitations for each analysis. Interview and company inputs will be identified as qualitative or self-reported evidence unless independently corroborated.
