# ELK Consortium

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **RFPs** | RFP 02 — Use Case Landscape & Competitive Positioning / RFP 05 — Government & Emerging Markets Entry Strategy |
| **Grant** | 110,000 ADA |

## Scope

Multi-entity coalition creating cross-vertical KPI frameworks across two research areas:

**RFP 02**: Map current and emerging use cases on Cardano and benchmark against comparable deployments on peer chains, identifying gaps, differentiated strengths, and positioning opportunities.

**RFP 05**: Develop entry strategies for Cardano in government and emerging market contexts, segmented by region and vertical, covering the regulatory landscape, key stakeholders, and recommended go-to-market approaches.
