## Agenda and material presented

The project-specific segment covered:

1. the study purpose and core-scope boundary;
2. the five research questions on buyer demand, Cardano technical properties, ecosystem activity, alternatives and positioning;
3. the mixed-method approach combining desk research, structured interviews and claim verification;
4. the credibility-ledger approach and separation of documented fact, respondent evidence, inference and vendor judgment;
5. the stakeholder groups and neutral, problem-first interview approach; and
6. committee challenge, target-list review and coordination for the next phase.

### Presentation summary

The project lead explained that the work would assess whether Cardano has a credible commercial position in AI rather than validate a predetermined narrative. The presentation identified five lines of enquiry:

- buyer problems, use cases and demand;
- the relevance and evidence for Cardano technical properties;
- current Cardano AI activity and capability;
- comparison with specialised AI networks, protocols and non-chain alternatives; and
- synthesis into a supported positioning and adoption pathway, if warranted.

The project lead described a mixed-method design using desk research, source gathering, structured interviews or written responses and a credibility ledger. The stakeholder universe presented included institutional buyers and adopters, AI-agent builders and platforms, delivery and infrastructure partners, Cardano ecosystem participants, independent or non-Cardano experts and the commissioning committee.
