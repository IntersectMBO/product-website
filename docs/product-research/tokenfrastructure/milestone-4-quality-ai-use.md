# Quality assurance and AI use

## Written-output coverage

| Output | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Decision memo | Final recommendation, conditions, actions, confidence and exclusions |
| Full evidence report | Method, sources, respondent categories, decision questions, findings, limitations and confidence labels |
| Public summary | Concise non-confidential account of the research and conclusions |
| Cross-RFP handoff memo | Findings routed to adjacent workstreams with explicit scope boundaries |
| Supporting matrices | Twelve scored use cases, priority records, adoption pathways, audience and competitive analysis, activity evidence, narrative tests and decision gates |
| Editable workbook | Twelve worksheets covering the analytical tables, limitations, actions and source register |
| Research-to-action pathway | Next engagements, dependencies, evidence thresholds and stop conditions |
| Source register | 75 public and technical entries with dates, evidence type, independence, quality and source URLs |

## Evidence controls

- The final evidence cut is 29 October 2026. The package date is 18 November 2026.
- The research uses 33 qualifying field records across six stakeholder segments. Five records concern institutional buyers and adopters; the sample supports qualitative findings rather than market-prevalence estimates.
- Dynamic activity figures retain their observation dates and provider or platform attribution.
- Weighted use-case scores follow the Milestone 1 rubric.
- Capability, observable activity, commercial adoption and strategic inference are distinguished throughout the research.
- Decision questions include conclusions, confidence, actions and material limitations.
- Priority recommendations state the relevant roles, evidence, remaining uncertainty and reversal conditions.
- Negative findings, alternatives and no-go conclusions remain visible.
- The optional proposal add-ons remain outside the research scope.

## Document verification

The submission was checked for relative links, evidence identifiers, use-case score arithmetic, consistency between the Markdown scoring table and the editable workbook, and inclusion of the four written deliverables. The repository build and packaged-file checks are documented in the submission materials.

## AI-use disclosure

AI tools assisted with source triage, research organisation, comparison structures, consistency checks, drafting and preparation of the submission files. They were not treated as evidence. Research judgments, respondent qualification, confidence, scores and recommendations remain the responsibility of IEI/TKNFRA.

## Retention and confidentiality

IEI/TKNFRA retains controlled interview notes, the detailed attribution ledger and supporting research records for at least twelve months. Public materials use anonymised segment-level synthesis unless a source is already public or attribution is permitted.

The public files exclude respondent identities, raw notes, organisation-to-response mappings and commercially sensitive information.
