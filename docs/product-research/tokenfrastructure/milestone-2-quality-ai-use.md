# Milestone 2 Quality, Confidentiality and AI Use

## Quality assurance

- Evidence is classified as documented fact, respondent evidence, analytical inference or vendor judgment.
- Material claims retain supporting evidence, counterevidence or limitation, confidence and a next test.
- Provider-reported metrics are labelled and are not treated as independent proof.
- Transaction or activity counts are not equated with adoption, revenue, retention or successful outcomes.
- Cardano dependency is assessed separately from Cardano association.
- Non-blockchain alternatives and competitor evidence are retained.
- Dates are recorded for dynamic sources, which will be refreshed before final publication.
- Human review is required before any statement enters a contractual deliverable.

## Confidentiality and attribution

Respondent names, raw notes, commercially sensitive information and organization-to-response mappings are retained in the controlled project record. Public reporting uses anonymized segment-level evidence unless explicit attribution is permitted. Naming a source organization in the public desk-source register does not imply that it participated in or endorsed the study.

This treatment follows the proposal commitment that respondents are informed of the research purpose and use of their input, that recording occurs only with consent, and that raw notes and commercially sensitive information are not published.

## AI-use disclosure

AI tools assisted with research organization, comparison structures, source triage and drafting. They did not determine evidence credibility, respondent qualification, contractual completion or conclusions. The IEI/TKNFRA research team remains responsible for source selection, validation, interpretation, quality control and all submitted text.

## Scope control

The work is limited to the signed core engagement. The optional extended interview package, deep verification of a named deployment and six-month monitoring are excluded. Findings and recommendations are intentionally not advanced into this Milestone 2 public evidence record because they are outputs of Milestones 3 and 4.
