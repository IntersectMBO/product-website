## Evidence base

The final synthesis draws on 75 public and technical source entries, 38 controlled claim records, eight comparison cases, twelve Cardano or Cardano-adjacent cases, twelve scored use cases and 33 qualifying field records across six stakeholder segments.

The field records form a purposive qualitative sample. They support analysis of buyer requirements, objections and delivery conditions; they do not establish market prevalence or product-market fit.

## Final recommendation

The recommendation is a **conditional go** for a narrow Cardano position in assurance and conditional settlement for open AI-agent services. The strongest candidates are escrowed service purchase, conditional API or data payments and minimal action or payment receipts.

Further investment depends on independent buyer demand, integration through existing standards and evidence that the Cardano-based control improves a purchase, risk or dispute outcome. The findings do not support a broad AI-chain positioning, machine-payment leadership, or a general compute or model marketplace strategy.

## Scope and evidence handling

The submission covers the signed 76,000 ADA core research engagement. Optional extended interviews, deep verification of a named deployment and six-month monitoring are outside that scope.

Public materials include dated sources, anonymised field synthesis, confidence, counterevidence, limitations and decision conditions. Respondent identities, raw notes, organisation-to-response mappings, commercially sensitive information and the exact controlled attribution ledger remain in the controlled research record for at least twelve months.
