## Contractual scope

The services for Milestone 2 are the structured gathering and logging of primary and secondary evidence, including credibility assessment, competitive teardown, activity-evidence review and interviews, with a mid-point checkpoint against the agreed framework.

The required deliverables are a populated credibility ledger, competitive teardown, activity-evidence review, interview log and preliminary synthesis, and a mid-point checkpoint note. Acceptance requires evidence of progress, adherence to the methodology, complete documents and acceptable evidence quality.

## Completion statement

The Milestone 2 evidence-gathering baseline is complete. The research base contains sufficient secondary research and field evidence to proceed to Milestone 3 synthesis:

- 66 named public, contractual and technical sources;
- 38 populated claim records with support, counterevidence, evidence status, confidence and a next test;
- 8 comparative cases and 12 Cardano or Cardano-adjacent activity cases;
- 33 qualifying interviews and research discussions gathered through conferences, direct calls and professional outreach;
- a balanced core analytical sample of 25 records, meeting the proposal commitment of 15–25 structured interviews, plus 8 supplementary qualifying discussions; and
- a completed written checkpoint against the Milestone 1 framework.

Further interviews may be added over the next few days where they strengthen coverage or corroboration. They are reinforcement only and are not a condition of Milestone 2 completion.

## Publication boundary

This PR provides a complete public assurance record for Milestone 2. It deliberately does not publish respondent identities, raw notes, substantive findings or the exact claim-to-source attribution ledger. Those controlled records are retained by IEI/TKNFRA in accordance with the proposal's confidentiality and data-handling commitments. Findings, matrices and recommendations belong to the contracted Milestone 3 synthesis and Milestone 4 final outputs.

## Scope boundary

This submission concerns the signed 76,000 ADA core engagement only. No optional extended-interview package, deep verification of a named deployment, pilot implementation or six-month monitoring is claimed. The 8 supplementary discussions arose within ordinary core fieldwork and do not constitute the separately priced optional interview add-on.
