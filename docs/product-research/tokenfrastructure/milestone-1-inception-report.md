## 1. Purpose and Scope

The study will assess whether AI can support a credible commercial positioning for Cardano, for which use cases and audiences, and under which technical, adoption, and evidence conditions. The research will test the proposed institutional-grade agent integrity, accountability, and settlement position as a hypothesis rather than assume it as a conclusion.

This inception baseline covers the 76,000 ADA core scope stated in the signed agreement. It is designed to produce evidence-based findings on demand, technical credibility, current Cardano activity, competitive position, adoption pathways, and the resulting strategic recommendation.

### Core Research Questions

- Which AI-related problems and use cases present credible demand from identifiable buyers or adopters?
- Which Cardano technical properties are relevant to those problems, and what evidence supports the associated claims?
- What AI-related activity and capability currently exist within the Cardano ecosystem?
- How does Cardano compare with specialised AI chains, relevant protocols, and non-chain alternatives for the shortlisted use cases?
- What positioning and adoption pathway, if any, is supported by the evidence?

### Scope Boundary

- **Included:** the research and decision outputs within the signed 76,000 ADA core scope.
- **Excluded optional add-ons:** the extended institutional interview base, deep verification of one named deployment, and six-month follow-on tracking of recommendation conditions.
- **Also excluded:** AI product or protocol development, pilot execution, implementation work, and technical-roadmap authorship.

## 2. Research Plan and Methodology

The project will use a mixed-method, evidence-first design. Desk research will be combined with targeted primary research and verification of material technical, market, and ecosystem claims. The method will distinguish documented fact, respondent evidence, analytical inference, and vendor judgment.

| Research component | Method |
|--------------------|--------|
| **Demand and buyer evidence** | Review buyer problems, adoption conditions, decision criteria, and credible use cases through public evidence and targeted interviews. |
| **Competitive position** | Compare Cardano with relevant specialised AI networks, protocols, and non-chain alternatives at the use-case level. |
| **Technical credibility** | Test material claims concerning determinism, formal methods, identity, auditability, privacy, and settlement against inspectable evidence. |
| **Synthesis** | Integrate the evidence into a supported positioning assessment and a go, conditional-go, monitor, or no-go recommendation. |

### Initial Source Groups

- **Cardano and project evidence:** official documentation, technical artifacts, ecosystem project materials, usage evidence, and other inspectable sources.
- **Competitive evidence:** official technical and commercial materials for relevant AI networks, protocols, and non-chain alternatives.
- **Market and adoption evidence:** documented deployments, buyer announcements, procurement or adoption cases, and credible market research.
- **Standards and protocol evidence:** relevant sources concerning agent payments, identity, authorisation, auditability, settlement, and compliance.
- **Primary evidence:** structured interviews and expert discussions across the stakeholder groups identified below.

## 3. Evidence Framework and Credibility Ledger

Material claims will be recorded in a credibility ledger so that the basis, strength, and limits of each conclusion remain visible. Evidence that cannot be independently inspected will be identified as such and weighted accordingly.

| Ledger field | Purpose |
|--------------|---------|
| **Claim or question** | The proposition being tested and the research question it informs. |
| **Source** | Source reference, source type, and date. |
| **Evidence status** | Documented, respondent-reported, inferred, or vendor judgment. |
| **Validation** | Whether and how the evidence has been corroborated. |
| **Independence** | Cardano-connected, independent, or mixed evidence. |
| **Confidence** | High, medium, or low, based on source quality and consistency. |
| **Limitations** | Known gaps, contradictions, access constraints, or conditions. |

### Credibility Standards

- Material conclusions will be traceable to identified evidence or clearly labelled judgment.
- Technical capability will be distinguished from demonstrated deployment or adoption.
- Contradictory evidence and material limitations will be retained in the analysis.
- Confidence will reflect evidence quality, independence, recency, and corroboration.

### AI-use Approach

AI tools may support source discovery, organisation, coding, and drafting. They will not be treated as authoritative evidence. Material outputs will be reviewed by the project team, source references will be checked, and confidential or personal information will not be entered into unapproved tools. Material AI assistance will be disclosed where relevant.

## 4. Stakeholder and Audience Segmentation

| Segment | Research contribution |
|---------|-----------------------|
| **Institutional buyers and adopters** | Demand, decision criteria, risk, procurement, and evidence thresholds. |
| **AI-agent builders and platforms** | Use-case requirements, integration conditions, and infrastructure alternatives. |
| **Delivery and infrastructure partners** | Delivery feasibility, dependencies, and adoption routes. |
| **Cardano ecosystem and technical contributors** | Current activity, technical evidence, and ecosystem capability. |
| **Independent and non-Cardano experts** | Comparative evidence and control perspectives. |
| **CPC and Intersect** | Research commissioner, methodology alignment, and milestone review. |

## 5. Success Criteria and Milestone 1 Confirmation

- The research questions can be answered through a proportionate mix of primary and secondary evidence.
- Material findings can be traced through the credibility ledger to their supporting evidence and limitations.
- The analysis distinguishes technical capability, demonstrated activity, commercial demand, and strategic inference.
- The stakeholder groups provide sufficient perspectives to assess buyer credibility, delivery feasibility, and competitive position.
- The final recommendation is supported by evidence and can distinguish go, conditional-go, monitor, and no-go outcomes.

This report consolidates the contractual Milestone 1 elements: the inception report, research plan and methodology, evidence framework and credibility-ledger structure, and stakeholder and audience segmentation. The methodology may be refined following CPC and Intersect review without expanding the signed core scope.
