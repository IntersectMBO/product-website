## Plan for Next Milestone

Milestone 2 - Evidence Gathering is due on 15 September 2026. The next phase will gather and log primary and secondary evidence; assess evidence credibility; compare relevant competitors and alternatives; review Cardano activity evidence; conduct targeted interviews; and complete a mid-point checkpoint against the agreed framework.

## Public Communication Summary

**Project purpose.** The project assesses whether AI can support a credible commercial position for Cardano, for which use cases and audiences, and under which technical, adoption, and evidence conditions.

**Milestone achievement.** Milestone 1 established the research questions, research plan and methodology, evidence framework and credibility-ledger structure, and stakeholder and audience segmentation for the study.

**Key takeaway.** The research tests Cardano's proposed AI positioning as a hypothesis rather than assuming it as a conclusion. Later recommendations will distinguish technical capability, demonstrated activity, commercial demand, and strategic inference.

**What comes next.** The next phase gathers and compares primary and secondary evidence across buyer demand, Cardano activity, technical credibility, and relevant alternatives.
