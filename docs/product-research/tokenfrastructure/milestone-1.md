## Summary

Milestone 1 establishes the research questions, evidence-first methodology, named source baseline, initial hypotheses, repeatable scoring rubric, credibility-ledger structure, success criteria and actionable stakeholder universe for the study. The work tests Cardano's proposed AI commercial position as a hypothesis rather than assuming it as a conclusion.

All evidence relied on for this review is contained in this pull request as Markdown. Administrative submission channels and private working files are not relied on as acceptance evidence.

## Milestone description

### Description of the services from Appendix A

Refinement and validation of the research questions, evidence framework and success criteria; agreement of methodology, source list and audience segments; confirmation of the credibility standards and AI-use approach for the Project.

### Deliverables from Appendix A

Inception report; agreed research plan and methodology; evidence framework and credibility ledger structure; stakeholder and audience segmentation.

The proposal's Milestone 1 execution baseline also called for confirmed interview targets, a rubric, an initial claim list and kick-off with the committee. In this submission, **confirmed interview targets** means an agreed organisation-level recruitment universe, not a claim that any organisation or individual has accepted an invitation.

## Plan for next milestone

Milestone 2 - Evidence Gathering is due on 15 September 2026. The next phase gathers and logs primary and secondary evidence, assesses credibility, compares relevant competitors and alternatives, reviews Cardano activity, conducts targeted interviews and completes a mid-point checkpoint against the agreed framework.

The contractual outputs are a populated credibility ledger, competitive teardown, activity-evidence review, interview log, preliminary synthesis and mid-point checkpoint note. These are not claimed as Milestone 1 outputs.

## Public communication summary

**Project purpose.** The project assesses whether AI can support a credible commercial position for Cardano, for which use cases and audiences, and under which technical, adoption and evidence conditions.

**Milestone achievement.** Milestone 1 establishes the research questions, method, 50-source baseline, initial hypotheses, assessment rubric, credibility-ledger structure, stakeholder segmentation, 50-organisation recruitment universe and CPC kick-off record.

**Key takeaway.** The research tests Cardano's proposed AI position as a hypothesis. Later recommendations will distinguish technical capability, demonstrated activity, commercial demand and strategic inference, including monitor and no-go outcomes.

**What comes next.** Milestone 2 populates the evidence and interview records and produces the first comparative findings.
