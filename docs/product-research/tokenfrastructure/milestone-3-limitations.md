# Limitations statement

| ID | Limitation | Effect | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L01 | The 33 field records form a purposive professional sample, not a statistically representative market survey. | The study can identify requirements and disconfirm claims but cannot estimate population prevalence. | Use qualitative convergence, preserve dissent and avoid percentage claims about buyer opinion. |
| L02 | Field interactions vary from structured calls to focused conference and LinkedIn discussions; not every record has a verbatim transcript. | Exact quotation and organization-level attribution are limited. | Use anonymized synthesis, retain raw records privately and make only claims supported by triangulation. |
| L03 | Only five field records sit in the institutional buyer and adopter segment. | Institutional demand and procurement conclusions have less breadth than protocol and builder findings. | Treat buyer conversion as a future funding gate rather than a completed market proof. |
| L04 | Protocol, marketplace and ecosystem metrics are provider-reported or dynamic. | Counts may include tests, retries, automated activity or concentrated users. | State dates and definitions and do not equate activity with revenue, retention or outcomes. |
| L05 | Masumi and Sokosumi activity is observable, but paid value, unique external buyers, repeat use and outcome quality are not independently disclosed. | Commercial adoption remains provisional. | Make transaction-quality evidence a condition for further funding. |
| L06 | Several standards and products remain draft, early or actively changing. | Competitive positions may shift quickly. | Version the findings and refresh dynamic facts during Milestone 4 finalization. |
| L07 | A public ledger proves integrity, ordering and settlement state, not the truth of off-chain data, user intent or service quality. | Auditability claims can be overstated. | Use minimal receipts and name the separate evaluation or dispute process. |
| L08 | Formal methods evidence applies to specified protocols, contracts and controls, not to all Cardano applications or AI behavior. | A platform-level reputation can be misrepresented as end-to-end assurance. | Name the verified artifact, property and boundary each time formal assurance is claimed. |
| L09 | Midnight, Veridian, NuNet and other adjacent assets may not depend on Cardano L1. | Ecosystem adjacency can be mistaken for Cardano adoption. | Trace the actual settlement, identity and data path before attributing value to Cardano. |
| L10 | No deep technical audit of a named deployment was performed. | Implementation, security and operating claims rely on public artifacts and interview evidence. | Keep the excluded optional deep-verification package outside the completion claim. |
| L11 | No paid market database, proprietary revenue dataset or six-month monitoring program was used. | Market sizing and longitudinal retention estimates remain limited. | Use observable decision gates and do not manufacture a total addressable market estimate. |
| L12 | AI tools assisted with source triage, structure and drafting. | Automated synthesis could propagate errors if not reviewed. | Human researchers selected sources, assessed credibility, checked claims and approved the submitted text. |
