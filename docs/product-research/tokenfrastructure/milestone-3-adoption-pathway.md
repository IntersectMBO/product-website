# Adoption-pathway matrix

| Pathway | Target | Value | Partners | Proof | Next step | Stop condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Escrowed agent services | AI API providers, agent platforms and B2B service marketplaces | Reduce non-delivery and counterparty risk for machine-delivered work | Masumi and Sokosumi; wallet or facilitator; stablecoin route; independent buyer channel | Paid external buyers, repeat use, completion, refund and dispute outcomes, integration effort | Define one narrow service workflow and buyer acceptance test | Buyers prefer plain payment or conventional marketplace escrow and will not pay or switch for conditional settlement |
| Payment-linked identity and reputation | Open agent marketplaces and cross-platform service networks | Connect accountable principal, delegated authority, service history and payment state | Credential or identity specialist; Masumi; authorization policy provider; independent validator | Revocation, Sybil resistance, privacy and measurable effect on counterparty choice | Specify the minimum interoperable claims and map them to W3C, OpenID and GLEIF standards | Federated credentials meet the need at lower cost or reputation signals do not change decisions |
| High-assurance agent receipts | Regulated B2B networks, audit and risk owners | Provide neutral integrity and ordering for approved authority, action, payment and outcome references | Enterprise integrator; risk or audit owner; privacy specialist; Cardano anchoring provider | Accepted minimal schema, privacy design, dispute value and operational ownership | Run a paper-based assurance design review with two independent control owners | Signed private logs or transparency services satisfy all material requirements |
| Open-standard distribution | Non-Cardano builders, agent-service providers and payment platforms | Make Cardano settlement reachable from existing agent stacks | x402 or MPP community; MCP or A2A tooling; wallet and stablecoin providers; Masumi | Documented compatibility, conformance, integration time and use by independent builders | Publish an interoperability matrix and reference integration requirements | Cardano-specific integration remains materially harder without a compensating buyer benefit |
| Privacy-adjacent settlement | Confidential or regulated workflows | Combine selective disclosure with settlement or minimal receipt anchoring | Midnight or privacy provider; enterprise buyer; interoperability specialist | End-to-end data flow, performance, governance and buyer acceptance | Hand off the architecture questions to the adjacent privacy workstream | Interoperability is impractical or public verifiability is unnecessary |

These pathways are research conclusions, not implementation commitments under the current contract.
