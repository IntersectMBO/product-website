## Scope

Highest-scoring proposal in the evaluation. Research examines institutional-grade agent integrity on Cardano — assessing the commercial opportunity for AI use cases where Cardano's properties (decentralisation, verifiability, programmability) offer genuine differentiation, producing validated positioning and go-to-market recommendations.
