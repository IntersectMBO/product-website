# Milestone 2 Competitive and Activity Review Coverage

This public coverage record confirms the completed scope of the competitive teardown and Cardano activity-evidence review. Findings and verdicts are reserved for Milestone 3.

## Competitive teardown

Eight comparison cases were assessed against a consistent structure covering the problem addressed, architecture, evidence class, maturity, buyer relevance, distribution, alternatives, limits and Cardano implications.

| # | Comparison case | Primary comparison layer |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Masumi and Sōkosumi on Cardano | Agent services, identity and conditional settlement |
| 2 | x402 on Solana and Base | Accountless machine payments and distribution |
| 3 | MPP and Tempo | Rail-agnostic machine payments and sessions |
| 4 | UCP, ACP and AP2 | Discovery, checkout, mandates and merchant control |
| 5 | Visa and Mastercard agent payments | Network identity, permissions and payment controls |
| 6 | ERC-8004 and ERC-8183 | Agent registries, reputation and agentic commerce |
| 7 | Bittensor, Akash and NuNet | AI markets and decentralized compute |
| 8 | W3C VC, OpenID4VP, C2PA and Veridian | Identity, credentials and provenance |

The comparative record also applies the agreed scoring dimensions to 12 candidate use cases. Scores are analytical aids rather than recommendations, and final prioritization is not published at this stage.

## Cardano activity-evidence review

Twelve Cardano or Cardano-adjacent cases were reviewed. Each controlled record distinguishes product availability, observable activity, provider claims, Cardano dependency, commercial signal, confidence and the validation still required.

| # | Case reviewed | Review focus |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Masumi | Agent registry, identity, escrow and service records |
| 2 | Sōkosumi | Agent-service marketplace and operational workflow |
| 3 | Cardano x402 scheme | Machine-payment implementation paths |
| 4 | Iagon | Decentralized storage and compute activity |
| 5 | SingularityNET | Current Cardano activity and dependency |
| 6 | NuNet | Compute orchestration and Cardano relationship |
| 7 | Rejuve.AI | Product activity and chain dependency |
| 8 | Veridian | Verifiable identity capability and chain dependency |
| 9 | Midnight | Privacy capability and adjacent-network pathway |
| 10 | Hydra | Scaling capability and relevant deployment evidence |
| 11 | UVerify and notary applications | Tamper-evident anchoring and receipts |
| 12 | Bending AI and Hizz | Application presence and adoption evidence |

## Completion test

The reviews are complete for Milestone 2 because the agreed cases, comparison dimensions, supporting and disconfirming evidence, confidence controls and follow-up tests are populated. Fast-moving metrics will be refreshed during synthesis, which reinforces rather than reopens the evidence-gathering milestone.
