# Audience-credibility matrix

| Audience | Credible claim | Avoid | Evidence required | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Institutional risk, audit and compliance | Cardano can anchor a minimal, tamper-evident record of approved authority, settlement state and service receipt across organizations. | Cardano proves agent intent, result correctness or regulatory compliance. | Mapped control objective, privacy assessment, named accountable operator and accepted receipt schema. | High |
| AI-agent builders and platforms | Masumi can add escrow, refunds and persistent receipts to an existing agent service without replacing its framework. | Builders must adopt a Cardano-native agent stack or proprietary standard. | Documented SDK path, low integration time, reliable recovery and production settlement assets. | High |
| Payments and commerce infrastructure | Cardano can complement x402 or other payment initiation when delivery risk requires conditional release and evidence. | Cardano leads machine-payment distribution or should replace card and stablecoin networks. | Facilitator and wallet support, reconciliation, compliance, refunds and measurable merchant or service-provider demand. | High |
| AI API and service providers | Conditional payment can reduce non-payment and non-delivery risk for higher-value digital services. | Every API call benefits from escrow or on-chain logging. | Paid conversion, repeat use, failure rate, refund behavior and unit economics versus plain x402 or subscriptions. | High |
| Standards, identity and security communities | Cardano can act as one interoperable trust and settlement anchor beneath open identity and authorization standards. | Cardano should define a universal agent identity, reputation or authorization standard. | Standards mapping, revocation and privacy model, conformance tests and neutral governance participation. | High |
| Cardano ecosystem and governance | A focused assurance wedge has a live product base and could create transaction, stablecoin and developer activity if independent demand is proven. | Directory listings or transaction counts already establish a strategic AI vertical. | External paying buyers, repeat transactions, production integrations and transparent activity definitions. | High |
| Public sector and regulated institutions | A minimal shared receipt may support cross-organization accountability where no party should control the record. | Publishing full AI logs or personal data on-chain improves compliance. | Legal basis, data minimization, selective disclosure, accessibility, procurement and records-management approval. | Medium |
| Merchants and consumer platforms | Cardano may help in narrow cross-border or digital-service cases where existing rails do not manage delivery risk. | Mainstream agent checkout needs a public blockchain. | Incremental conversion or cost advantage with stable fraud, refund and customer-support outcomes. | Low |
