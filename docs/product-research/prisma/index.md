# PRISMA

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **RFP** | RFP 05 — Government & Emerging Markets Entry Strategy |
| **Grant** | 58,000 ADA |

## Scope

Regional adoption readiness framework for Africa and LATAM. Research assesses the regulatory, infrastructure, and stakeholder conditions for Cardano adoption across both regions, producing prioritised market entry recommendations segmented by country and vertical.

## Milestone Reports

Milestone reports submitted by PRISMA will appear here.
