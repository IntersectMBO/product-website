# Question Pack — Regulators & Policy

## Pack (fixed)

| Field | Value |
| :---- | :---- |

## Run — primary gates

### Regulation

* Which bodies have jurisdiction over blockchain, digital assets, payments, and data for **this play** in **this cluster**?
* What licensing, registration, or sandbox pathways exist today for **this use case** — and which are theoretical only?
* What is the current posture on stablecoins / crypto payments where **this play** needs them: permitted, restricted, prohibited, or unclear?
* How do data-protection and public-chain transparency rules interact for **this play**?
* What public-procurement rules would shape or block a government-facing version of **this play**?
* What FX or capital-control rules affect settlement or off-ramps for **this play**?
* Where must specialist local counsel be engaged before any go / conditional-go call on **this play**?

### Use cases

* Is the regulatory path for **this use case** comparatively clear, blocked, or contested in **this cluster**?
* Have any blockchain or digital-asset pilots close to **this play** been approved, rejected, or left in limbo — and on what grounds?

### Infrastructure

* Which compliance artifacts (KYC/AML, licensing proofs, SLAs) are non-negotiable before **this play** can run?
* Does identity / KYC infrastructure in this market support **this play**, or is it a hard dependency?

## Run — secondary gates

### Counterparties

* Who are the practical gatekeepers for non-objection or approval on **this play**?
* How reachable are they for an external ecosystem actor within 12–24 months?

### Regions

* Relative to peer jurisdictions, is **this cluster** more or less workable for **this use case** — and why?

### Cross-RFP / adjacent issues

* Which blockers of **this play** are pure regulatory/legal vs product, liquidity, or delivery-partner problems that belong elsewhere?

### Adoption (if a signal appears)

* Propose a CASS tier and evidence rationale — do not upgrade on intent alone.
