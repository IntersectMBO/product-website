## Run — primary gates

### Regions

* Is the problem behind **this use case** acute enough in **this cluster** that institutions are actively seeking new tools — not only discussing them?
* Where in or near **this cluster** would you *not* recommend this kind of initiative right now, and why?
* Compared with peer countries or neighbouring clusters, is institutional readiness for **this play** higher or lower?

### Use cases

* Does **this use case** have a live problem owner inside government today?
* Has **this use case** (or a close digital substitute) been tried before here — what failed or stalled?
* Would a public ledger / deterministic settlement / credentials actually change this problem — or would a conventional system suffice?

### Counterparties

* Who can approve or commission a pilot for **this play**?
* Whose non-objection is required before that pilot can proceed (ministry, regulator, procurement, treasury)?
* What route exists for an external partner to reach those actors within 12–24 months?
* Which intermediaries (donors, multilaterals, local integrators) actually open doors for **this play**?

### Regulation

* What licensing, data, or procurement rules would block or delay a pilot of **this play**?
* Is the current posture toward blockchain / digital assets Clear, Evolving, or Uncertain for **this use case**?
* Where would you insist local counsel be engaged before any commitment?

## Run — secondary gates

### Delivery model

* Who would build and operate a pilot for **this play** if approved — internal IT, preferred vendor, open tender, or donor-funded implementer?
* What capacity gaps would kill **this play** even if political will existed?

### Adoption

* What would count as success for a pilot of **this play** in your institution?
* What “interest” signals around **this play** should outsiders treat as weak (events, MoUs, panels)?
* If an adoption signal is claimed: propose a CASS tier and evidence rationale.

### Cross-RFP / adjacent issues

* Which blockers of **this play** are not regional-entry problems (stablecoin liquidity, wallet UX, enterprise compliance) and belong in other workstreams?
