## Run — primary gates

### Infrastructure

* Is there a stable-value instrument with usable liquidity for the volumes **this play** would need?
* Can users or institutions reliably off-ramp to local fiat at acceptable cost and compliance for **this play**?
* What wallet / account UX barriers block non-crypto users from completing the payment flow **this play** assumes?
* Does the rail work on basic mobile devices or intermittent connectivity where **this play** needs it?
* What identity / KYC stack do you require, and does it cover the population **this play** targets?

### Regulation

* What licences or permissions govern crypto-linked, stablecoin, or on-chain settlement activity for **this play**?
* Where is the regulatory path Clear / Evolving / Uncertain for **this use case** in **this cluster**?
* What would force you to refuse a partnership on **this play** even if commercially attractive?

### Use cases

* Is **this use case** actionable now in **this cluster**, or still aspirational?
* Where do current rails already solve the problem well enough that blockchain adds little to **this play**?

## Run — secondary gates

### Counterparties

* Who decides on partnerships or pilots involving digital assets for something like **this play** inside your organisation?
* Which external actors (regulators, schemes, aggregators) must be aligned before a live pilot of **this play**?

### Delivery model

* Would you build in-house, partner with a local integrator, or require a global vendor for any on-chain component of **this play**?

### Adoption

* What usage metrics would convince you a pilot of **this play** had real traction?
* Which announced “crypto payment” initiatives near **this play** were weak signals?
* If an adoption signal is claimed: propose a CASS tier and evidence rationale.

### Cross-RFP / adjacent issues

* Which gaps for **this play** are stablecoin-liquidity or enterprise-readiness problems that should be handed to adjacent RFPs?
