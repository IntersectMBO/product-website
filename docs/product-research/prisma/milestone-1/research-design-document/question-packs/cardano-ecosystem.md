# Question Pack — Cardano Ecosystem

## Pack (fixed)

| Field | Value |
| :---- | :---- |

## Run — primary gates

### Adoption

* Which local stories about **this play** are Tier 1–2 (production / funded pilot with owner) vs Tier 3–4 (working group / events / LoIs without owner)?
* What evidence would upgrade or downgrade a signal you currently cite for **this play**?
* Where has community enthusiasm been mistaken for institutional adoption around **this play**?
* Propose a CASS tier and evidence rationale for each signal discussed.

### Regions

* On comparative evidence — not narrative — does **this cluster** deserve near-term priority for **this use case**, or should it be monitored / deprioritised?
* Where is ecosystem presence strong but problem severity or counterparty access weak for **this play** (or the reverse)?

### Counterparties

* Which decision sponsors, regulators, or delivery partners for **this play** have you *actually* engaged (Interviewed / Warm Access / Referred)?
* Which oft-named institutions for **this play** are desk-identified or hypothetical only?
* What route to decision-makers works in practice for **this play** in **this cluster**?

## Run — secondary gates

### Use cases

* Does **this use case** have non-Cardano local demand in **this cluster**, or mainly Cardano-advocate demand?
* Where did a promising version of **this play** stall — and was the blocker regional, regulatory, product, or delivery?

### Delivery model

* Who can build and support a real pilot of **this play** locally today?
* What capacity gaps keep projects near **this play** at hackathon or demo stage?

### Infrastructure

* Which prerequisites (stablecoin, off-ramp, wallet UX, identity, mobile) are the binding constraints for **this play** in **this cluster**?

### Cross-RFP / adjacent issues

* Which findings about **this play** should leave RFP 05 and go to stablecoin, enterprise/RWA, delivery-partner, or product workstreams?
