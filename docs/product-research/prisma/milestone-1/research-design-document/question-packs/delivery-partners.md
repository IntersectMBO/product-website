## Run — primary gates

### Delivery model

* What can your organisation realistically build, operate, support, and scale for **this play** in **this cluster** within 12–24 months?
* Who typically owns the client relationship vs the technical build for projects like **this play**?
* What partner archetypes succeed locally for **this play** (global SI + local sub, pure local integrator, NGO + tech, bank-led)?
* Where have delivery partnerships failed on similar plays, and why?

### Infrastructure

* For **this play**: which prerequisites are Met / Partially Met / Unmet / Unknown today (stablecoin, off-ramp, wallet UX, mobile/offline, identity, compliance artifacts)?
* What SLA or compliance documentation do institutional clients demand that the local market cannot yet supply for **this play**?

### Adoption

* In projects you have shipped near **this play**, what evidence showed real adoption vs cosmetic launch?
* What KPIs would you accept as stop conditions for a pilot of **this play**?
* If an adoption signal is claimed: propose a CASS tier and evidence rationale.

## Run — secondary gates

### Use cases

* Does **this use case** have real paying or funded demand from non-crypto clients in **this cluster**?
* Where is blockchain a hard requirement vs a preference for **this play** — and when would a conventional stack win?

### Counterparties

* Which decision sponsors and procurement owners have you actually worked with on plays like this (lived experience, not aspiration)?
* What is the realistic path and timeline to a signed, funded pilot of **this play** with those actors?

### Regions

* In which sub-national or country contexts within or near **this cluster** is delivery for **this play** actually feasible vs overhyped?

### Cross-RFP / adjacent issues

* Which gaps for **this play** require product, stablecoin, or enterprise-readiness workstreams rather than a regional playbook alone?
