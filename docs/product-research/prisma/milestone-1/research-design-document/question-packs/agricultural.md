## Run — primary gates

### Use cases

* Where do farmer identity, market access, traceability, or payment problems create real losses that **this play** claims to address in **this cluster**?
* Have digital or blockchain tools close to **this use case** been tried in your network? What happened?
* What would need to be true for a Cardano-based version of **this play** to beat a conventional digital system?

### Counterparties

* Who can commission or block a pilot of **this play** affecting farmers or aggregators in this value chain?
* Which cooperatives, buyers, exporters, or ministries are reachable within 12–24 months for **this play**?
* Who holds trust with farmers that an external tech partner does not?

### Delivery model

* Who would enrol farmers, train users, and support the system after launch for **this play**?
* What local implementation capacity exists for **this play** — and what is missing?

### Adoption

* What would meaningful adoption look like for **this play**: recurring transactions, buyer dependency, certified lots, paid subscriptions?
* What activity looks like adoption but is not (one-off demos, NGO workshops, event registrations)?
* If an adoption signal is claimed: propose a CASS tier and evidence rationale.

## Run — secondary gates

### Infrastructure

* Do farmers and aggregators have the mobile connectivity, identity documents, and payment rails **this play** assumes?
* Where do offline, literacy, or agent-network constraints dominate for **this play**?

### Regulation

* What data, land, or payment rules affect **this play**?
* Where is legal enforceability of digital records weak enough to kill **this play**?

### Regions

* In which districts or country clusters is **this play** more viable than in neighbouring areas?

### Cross-RFP / adjacent issues

* Which blockers of **this play** are identity-product, stablecoin, or delivery-network issues rather than regional strategy?
