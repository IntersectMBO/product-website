## Run — primary gates

### Use cases

* Is the failure behind **this use case** acute enough in **this cluster** that your organisation would consider a new rail — not just a demo?
* Have you evaluated blockchain or digital-asset tools for something like **this play** before? What was decided and why?
* Would Cardano-specific properties (deterministic settlement, credentials, public auditability) matter for **this play**, or would any compliant digital system suffice?

### Counterparties

* Who inside your organisation can sponsor a pilot of **this play** (mandate + budget + decision rights)?
* Which government or regulator relationships are required before **this play** can proceed in **this cluster**?
* Which local partners do you already trust for last-mile delivery or beneficiary access on **this play**?

### Delivery model

* Who would implement and operate a pilot of **this play** day-to-day — your team, a local integrator, a global vendor, or a consortium?
* What delivery capacity is missing locally that has killed or delayed similar projects?

### Adoption

* What counts as real adoption for **this play**: recurring beneficiary payments, institutional dependency, audited workflow integration, or something else?
* Which “pilot” announcements near **this play** should be treated as weak signals?
* If an adoption signal is claimed: propose a CASS tier and evidence rationale.

## Run — secondary gates

### Regulation

* What compliance or donor constraints govern **this play** (payments, data, digital assets)?
* Where is the regulatory path unclear enough that you would not proceed without counsel?

### Infrastructure

* What must exist before **this play** is credible: mobile reach, identity, off-ramp, offline capability, privacy?
* Which of those are unmet in **this cluster**?

### Cross-RFP / adjacent issues

* Which blockers of **this play** are liquidity, wallet UX, or enterprise-compliance issues rather than regional entry strategy?
