# Question Packs

Interviewers do not walk into a call with a loose topic. They assemble equipment first: one play and one question pack. That assembled brief is what they take into the interview.

## The unit: play × pack

| Piece | Meaning |
| :---- | :---- |
| Use Case | The entry-play hypothesis under test (`region x use-case`) |
| Question Pack | One set of questions for the given [respondent type](/en/events/cpc/appendix/stakeholder-access#101-respondent-categories-and-access-basis) — i.e. which [decision gates](/en/events/cpc/executing/terminology/decision-gates) this person can best answer |
| Assembled brief | That play viewed through that pack |

## How to assemble

1. Name the play (`cluster × use case`) from the [Use-Case Hypothesis Register](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/research-design-document/use-case-hypothesis-register) (clusters listed in [Candidate Regions](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/research-design-document/candidate-regions)).
2. Choose the pack that matches the respondent type.
3. Clone a copy of that pack page and fill the Assemble fields.
4. Run [Interview Conduct](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/research-design-document/interview-guide/conduct) with the filled brief as your guide.
5. Tag findings to gates of *this play*; do not wander across unrelated verticals.

Do not force every gate. Prioritise the pack's primary gates; use secondary probes only when the respondent has direct knowledge.
