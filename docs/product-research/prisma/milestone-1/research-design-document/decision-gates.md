# Decision Gates

* Which regions should be prioritised for near-term government and emerging-market entry, based on comparative evidence rather than strategic narrative?

* Which use cases are credible entry plays in each priority region, and which are aspirational without current evidence of viability?

* Which counterparties are reachable within a 12–24 month horizon, and what route exists to decision-makers or implementation partners?

* What does the regulatory landscape look like per priority region, and what dependencies or blockers affect the entry play?

* What infrastructure prerequisites must be in place before action is credible, including stablecoin liquidity, off-ramp access, identity, privacy, mobile-first design, and delivery capacity?

* What partner and delivery model is required in each region, and who can build, operate, support, and scale the solution?

* What constitutes meaningful adoption in each play, and what should not be counted as evidence of progress?

* Which findings belong in adjacent RFPs or workstreams, and what cross-portfolio handoffs are required to keep scope clean?

# **7\. Decision Gate Mapping**

The table below maps each of the eight RFP 05 decision gates to the primary method, evidence source, deliverable, limitation, and decision enabled by this research.

| Decision Gate | Primary Method | Key Evidence Source | Core Deliverable | Decision Enabled |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Which regions should Cardano prioritise first? | RARF application; desk screening; stakeholder interviews | Regional market indicators; expert interviews; ecosystem data | Market Prioritisation Matrix | Sequence regional attention and funding; deprioritise or monitor low-scoring clusters |
| Which use cases are credible entry plays by region? | Use-case viability assessment; operator-level interviews; comparative desk research | Local practitioner interviews; competitive landscape; secondary market data | Use-Case Viability Assessment | Select leading use cases per region; reject unsupported vertical claims |
| Which counterparties are reachable on 12–24 month horizon? | Counterparty mapping; hub network engagement; stakeholder interviews | Interviewed contacts; PRISMA hub network; desk-identified institutional actors | Counterparty & Partner Access Map | Pursue partner engagement, intermediary strategy, or no-action |
| What does the regulatory landscape require per region? | Regulatory landscape mapping; policy desk research; local expert calls | Regulatory body publications; policy expert interviews; legal counsel (where accessible) | Regulatory Landscape Summaries | Define regulatory diligence, local counsel needs, go/no-go conditions |
| What infrastructure prerequisites must be in place? | Infrastructure prerequisite assessment; practitioner interviews | Mobile/digital infrastructure data; payment ecosystem analysis; delivery partner interviews | Infrastructure Prerequisite Assessment | Coordinate with stablecoin, enterprise/RWA, delivery partner, and interoperability workstreams |
| What partner and delivery model is required? | Partner archetype analysis; local delivery capacity review | Local system integrator interviews; hub network evidence; implementation partner mapping | Regional Entry Playbooks (delivery model component) | Define partner recruitment, local capability building, and pilot ownership requirements |
| What constitutes meaningful adoption vs. cosmetic activity? | CASS classification; adoption signal analysis | Primary research evidence; usage data where available; implementation actor interviews | Adoption Signal Framework (CASS) | Set pilot KPIs, funding triggers, and stop conditions |
| Which findings should go to adjacent RFPs? | Scope boundary analysis throughout research | Research findings across all workstreams | Cross-RFP Handoff Memo | Keep portfolio scope clean; route non-regional issues to the correct decision owners |
