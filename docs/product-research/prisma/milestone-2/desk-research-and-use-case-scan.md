# Desk Research Report, Use-Case Viability Register, and Adoption Signal Assessment

This report sets out the Phase 2 desk research findings, incumbent systems comparison, use-case hypothesis register, and adoption signal review across all eight candidate clusters. It applies the frameworks defined in Milestone 1 [Use-Case Hypothesis Register](../milestone-1/research-design-document/use-case-hypothesis-register.md), [Adoption Signals (CASS)](../milestone-1/research-design-document/adoption-signals.md), and [Question Packs](../milestone-1/research-design-document/question-packs/index.md) [`REF-72`].

Each use-case classification (`desk-supported`, `revised`, `contested-weak`, or `rejected-desk`) represents a provisional Phase 2 desk assessment structured for empirical testing and potential falsification during Phase 3 fieldwork (Weeks 7–12).

## 2. Comparative Assessment Against Local Incumbent Systems and Competing Rails

Mainnet readiness is only half the test; every screened market already runs national payment switches, state identity registries, enterprise resource planning (ERP) platforms, or competing blockchains. A public ledger is justified only where independent organisations must verify shared transactions or credentials across institutional or jurisdictional boundaries without relying on one central database administrator. Where a central bank switch, telecom operator, or industry association already provides instant, low-cost domestic service, introducing a public blockchain adds cost and compliance friction without solving a live operational problem. ## 3. Phase 2 Use-Case Hypothesis Register (`H1` to `H15`)

Testing Cardano's baseline (Section 1) against local incumbents (Section 2) yields updated desk dispositions for the regional use-case register. Milestone 1 [Use-Case Hypothesis Register](../milestone-1/research-design-document/use-case-hypothesis-register.md) logged twelve initial hypotheses (`H1` through `H12`) across seven clusters [`REF-72`]. To cover all eight candidate clusters from Milestone 1 [Candidate Regions](../milestone-1/candidate-regions.md), this register keeps `H1` through `H12` unchanged and adds three hypotheses for Horn / Ethiopia: `H13` (Remittances), `H14` (Identity and credentials), and `H15` (Public-service delivery and education records).

At the close of Phase 2 desk screening, the fifteen hypotheses fall into four provisional categories:

- **`desk-supported` (4 plays: `H2`, `H5`, `H6`, `H11`):** Secondary sources document an acute multi-party problem, an enacted legal pathway, and a verification gap not resolved by one centralised database. `H2`, `H5`, and `H6` advance to Phase 3 falsification; `H11` remains on desk monitoring as a European reference benchmark.
- **`revised` (4 plays: `H1`, `H4`, `H7`, `H12`):** The original framing runs into established domestic switches (NIBSS NIP, M-Pesa, Transferencias 3.0, PLACSP), so each hypothesis is narrowed to a B2B, cross-border, or sub-threshold audit niche. `H1`, `H4`, and `H7` advance to Phase 3; `H12` remains on desk monitoring.
- **`contested-weak` (4 plays: `H3`, `H8`, `H9`, `H10`):** Secondary evidence points to severe statutory, incumbent, or capital barriers. `H3` (Nigeria land registry) stays in scope for 1–2 diagnostic interviews in West Africa, while `H8` (Brazil), `H9` (Mexico), and `H10` (Andean) remain on `Monitor` with no Phase 3 fieldwork allocation.
- **`rejected-desk` (3 regional plays in Ethiopia: `H13`, `H14`, `H15`, logged alongside two cross-cutting exclusions `REJ-04` and `REJ-05` in Section 5):** Falsified at the desk gate by active central bank prohibitions (NBE crypto ban) and federal consolidation onto non-blockchain state infrastructure (Fayda MOSIP).
