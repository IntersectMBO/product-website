## Summary

Phase 2 regional screening and desk research is complete across all eight candidate market clusters established in Milestone 1. Applying the Milestone 1 [Regional Adoption Readiness Framework (RARF)](../milestone-1/research-design-document/adoption-readiness.md), [Cardano Adoption Signal Standard (CASS)](../milestone-1/research-design-document/adoption-signals.md), [Decision Gates](../milestone-1/research-design-document/decision-gates.md), and [Use-Case Hypothesis Register](../milestone-1/research-design-document/use-case-hypothesis-register.md), Phase 2 scored all eight clusters across ten readiness dimensions, benchmarked fifteen regional use-case hypotheses against domestic Web2 switches and competing blockchains, completed an eight-cluster statutory and regulatory scan, mapped 64 target institutions (81.25% independent non-Cardano), and published the Phase 3 coding schema, participant consent scripts, and fieldwork verification protocol.

Screening advances three emerging-market anchor hubs to Phase 3 primary fieldwork (Weeks 7–12) — **Buenos Aires Hub** (Southern Cone), **Nairobi Hub** (East Africa), and **Lagos Hub** (West Africa) — while retaining Iberia, Brazil, Andean, and Mexico on desk monitoring and classifying Horn / Ethiopia as `No-Go`. Because secondary research cannot confirm active institutional pilot budgets or live commercial settlement spreads against Cardano's USD 59.6M stablecoin liquidity (USD 0.00 in local-currency stablecoins and COTI's 31 December 2026 DJED wind-down), all Phase 2 ratings are provisional and paired with explicit falsification tests for Phase 3 interviews.

### Regional Screening Findings

1. **Three Priority Emerging-Market Anchor Hubs Advance to Phase 3 Fieldwork (3 Regional Hubs / 9 Local Teams / 42–51 Target Interviews):**
   - **Priority 1 — Southern Cone (`Buenos Aires Hub` | Weighted RARF 3.30 / 5.00 | 66.0 / 100 | Rank 1, 10/10 dims >= 3):** Ranks first across baseline, equal-weight, hub-independent, and acute-need sensitivity models. Persistent FX spreads drive 60%+ stablecoin volume share in Argentina [`REF-37`, `REF-56`], while `DNU 70/2023`, `CNV RG 1172/2026`, and Uruguay's `Law 20.345` [`REF-31`, `REF-32`, `REF-39`] enable B2B cooperative USD escrow (`H6`) and Mercosur credential-linked contractor settlement (`H7`) across 14–17 planned interviews (`AR`, `UY`, `PY`).
   - **Priority 2 — East Africa (`Nairobi Hub` | Weighted RARF 3.15 / 5.00 | 63.0 / 100 | Rank 2 tie, 10/10 dims >= 3):** Clears `>= 3.0` on all ten readiness dimensions. Enacted VASP regimes in Kenya (`VASP Act 2025` + `10%` fee excise) and Rwanda (`Law 023/2026`) [`REF-16`, `REF-17`, `REF-23`] pair with the `30 December 2026` `EUDR` export traceability mandate [`REF-22`] across 13–16 planned interviews (`KE`, `RW`, `UG`) testing `H5` and `H4`.
   - **Priority 3 — West Africa (`Lagos Hub` | Weighted RARF 3.15 / 5.00 | 63.0 / 100 | Rank 2 tie, 9/10 dims >= 3):** Pairs ~8.46% regional remittance friction [`REF-55`] with enacted VASP licensing in Nigeria (`ISA 2025`, `SEC ARIP`, `Circular 26-1`) and Ghana (`Act 1154`) [`REF-01`, `REF-02`, `REF-12`]. Anchoring Nigeria alongside the paired Accra/Ghana corridor, Phase 3 schedules 15–18 interviews (14–16 across `H1` and `H2` plus 1–2 diagnostic `H3` land-registry calls).
2. **Four Candidate Clusters Retained on Desk Monitoring (`Monitor`):**
   - **Iberia (`Spain — Barcelona` — 3.15 / 5.00 | 8/10 dims >= 3):** Strong EU statutory clarity (`MiCA`, `eIDAS 2.0`, `EDPB 02/2025 v2.0`, `LCSP Art. 118`) [`REF-49`–`REF-53`], but low domestic payment friction (`D1 = 2` under `SEPA Instant` and `Bizum`) [`REF-54`]; retained as a European regulatory reference benchmark.
   - **Brazil (3.00), Andean (2.60), and Mexico (2.55):** Held at desk stage due to domestic payment saturation and regulatory or capital barriers (details in [Market Prioritisation Matrix](./market-prioritisation-matrix.md) §4.5).
3. **One Candidate Cluster Excluded from Near-Term Entry (`No-Go`):**
   - **Horn / Ethiopia (2.00 / 5.00 | 40.0 / 100 — `No-Go`):** Excluded due to the `NBE` May 2022 crypto prohibition [`REF-26`] and federal consolidation onto non-blockchain `Fayda` (`MOSIP`, ~39M–50M enrolled) [`REF-28`].
