## 3. Priority Deep-Dive Hub Selection for Phase 3 Fieldwork

Applying the five advancement gates from Milestone 1 [Region Selection](../milestone-1/research-design-document/region-selection.md) and the desk scoring and sensitivity checks in the [Regional Market Prioritisation Matrix](./market-prioritisation-matrix.md) (§2–§3), three priority emerging-market anchor hubs (3 Local Project Teams per hub = 9 Local Teams) advance to Phase 3 fieldwork (Weeks 7–12). Each hub leads fieldwork in its anchor market while coordinating cross-border outreach to neighbouring countries within its cluster:

1. **Priority Emerging-Market Playbook Hub 1 — Southern Cone (Buenos Aires Hub | RARF 3.30 / 5.00 | 66.0 / 100 | Rank 1, 10/10 dims >= 3):**
   - *Operational Scope:* Coordinated by PRISMA's LATAM Regional Intelligence and Buenos Aires Hub leads ([Proposal](../proposal.md) §16.1), anchoring primary outreach in Argentina (`CABA`, cooperative, and `PSAV` ecosystems) and cross-border Mercosur interviews into Uruguay and Paraguay.
   - *Phase 3 Allocation:* 14–17 primary interviews across 3 Local Project Teams testing cooperative and SME programmable USD escrow (`H6`) and Mercosur contractor and credential-linked settlement (`H7`) (statutory and market basis in [Market Prioritisation Matrix](./market-prioritisation-matrix.md) §4.1).
2. **Priority Emerging-Market Playbook Hub 2 — East Africa (Nairobi Hub | RARF 3.15 / 5.00 | 63.0 / 100 | Rank 2 tie, 10/10 dims >= 3):**
   - *Operational Scope:* Coordinated by the Nairobi Regional Hub and Sub-Saharan Africa research network ([Proposal](../proposal.md) §16.1 & §17.1), anchoring Kenya fieldwork and EAC corridor outreach into Rwanda and Uganda.
   - *Phase 3 Allocation:* 13–16 primary interviews across 3 Local Project Teams testing `EUDR` (`30 December 2026` go-live) agricultural export traceability (`H5`) and cross-border EAC and humanitarian settlement (`H4`) (detailed in [Market Prioritisation Matrix](./market-prioritisation-matrix.md) §4.2).
3. **Priority Emerging-Market Playbook Hub 3 — West Africa (Lagos Hub | RARF 3.15 / 5.00 | 63.0 / 100 | Rank 2 tie, 9/10 dims >= 3):**
   - *Operational Scope:* Coordinated by the Lagos Regional Hub and West Africa regulatory and research leads alongside Wada Hub Network coordinators in Accra ([Proposal](../proposal.md) §16.1 & §17.2), anchoring Nigeria (`136M+` NIN records, `USD 92.1B` Chainalysis 2025 inflows, `ISA 2025`, `SEC Circular 26-1`) [`REF-01`, `REF-09`, `REF-56`] and the paired Ghana (`Act 1154`) corridor.    - *Phase 3 Allocation:* 15–18 primary interviews across 3 Local Project Teams: 14–16 interviews across wholesale B2B/MTO corridor settlement (`H1`) and multi-issuer verifiable credentials (`H2`), plus 1–2 diagnostic falsification calls on state land-registry constraints (`H3`, `Land Use Act 1978 s. 22`) [`REF-07`].

### Quality Assurance Controls for Regional Hub Execution

Four operational controls govern fieldwork completion across all regional teams during Weeks 7–12:

1. **Verification Prerequisite for Completed Interviews:** Only interviews passing the six-step verification protocol ([Schema Protocol](./phase-2-schema-and-protocol-update.md) §4) count toward Milestone 3 regional interview quotas and synthesis outputs.
2. **Confidential Participant Protection & Non-PII Quota Verification:** `Private` and `Research` interviews remain strictly inside encrypted local hub vaults and log a signed, non-PII `audit_ledger_entry` record ([Schema Protocol](./phase-2-schema-and-protocol-update.md) §3.2) so confidential respondents count equally toward hub quotas without pressure for public attribution. Public playbooks cite only consent-cleared public or anonymous records.
3. **Sample Balance & Independence Controls:** A regional hub's interview sample counts as complete only when it spans at least four distinct Question Pack categories, includes at least two completed Non-Cardano Counterweight interviews, and keeps both Category 7 (external regional Cardano builders and implementers) and total `coi_flag: true` participants at or below 25% (target `<= 20%`).
4. **Weekly Cross-Hub Calibration:** Consent-cleared summaries and anonymised quota telemetry are reviewed weekly by the Principal Investigator and Co-PI / Project Lead to audit coding consistency across Buenos Aires, Nairobi, and Lagos.
