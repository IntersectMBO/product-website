# Statutory, Institutional and Technical Reference Registry

This registry indexes the 72 statutory, regulatory, multilateral, industry, and technical sources cited across the Milestone 2 regional screening deliverables for RFP 05 — Government & Emerging Markets Entry Strategy (verified as of November 2026).

## 1. Source Classification and Evidence Governance

Under the Milestone 1 [Data Governance Protocol](../milestone-1/research-design-document/data.md), each entry is classified across three dimensions:

1. **Source Category:**
   - `Public Secondary - Statutory / Regulatory`: Enacted parliamentary statutes, presidential decrees, central bank circulars, securities commission rulebooks, and data-protection authority guidance.
   - `Public Secondary - Multilateral / Official Statistics`: World Bank, IMF, BIS, GSMA, national identity agencies, and national payment-switch statistical bulletins.
   - `Public Secondary - Industry / Competitor Telemetry`: Independent market analytics (Chainalysis, DeFiLlama) and technical specifications of existing Web2 platforms or competing blockchains.
   - `Public Secondary - Ecosystem / Applicant Technical`: Published Cardano Improvement Proposals (CIPs), open-source middleware repositories (Hyperledger Identus, Veridian, Midnight, Orcfax), and PRISMA research governance documentation.
2. **Independence Classification:**
   - `Independent`: Produced by a legislature, regulator, multilateral agency, national payment switch, or non-Cardano commercial operator with zero financial interest in Cardano adoption.
   - `Ecosystem Primary`: Produced by a Cardano ecosystem entity or protocol team. Used to verify technical capabilities or evaluate historical ecosystem claims, never as standalone proof of regional market demand.
   - `Applicant Technical`: Produced by Aspecting OÜ (trading as Prisma) or its open-source engineering contributors.
3. **Desk Confidence Calibration:**
   - Statutory texts and central bank circulars carry `Medium (Secondary Data Only)` confidence on enacted legal rules, paired with `Low (Pre-Interview Desk Estimate)` confidence on practical administrative enforcement and commercial bank compliance posture until tested in Phase 3 interviews.
