# Stakeholder Ecosystem and Counterparty Mapping Report

This report translates the Phase 2 regional desk screening into an institutional recruitment slate for Phase 3 fieldwork (Weeks 7–12), applying the sampling framework from Milestone 1 [Stakeholder Recruitment Plan](../milestone-1/research-design-document/stakeholder-recruitment-plan.md) and [Bias Control](../milestone-1/research-design-document/bias-control.md) [`REF-72`].

All 64 entries represent Phase 2 pre-interview institutional mappings. Primary interviews begin in Phase 3 across the three priority emerging-market anchor hubs: Buenos Aires Hub, Nairobi Hub, and Lagos Hub.

---

## 1. Sampling Methodology and Recruitment Structure

To ensure Phase 3 fieldwork captures operational, regulatory, and technical perspectives across each advancing market rather than a single stakeholder tier, the recruitment slate follows five design rules:

1. **Five-Tier Access Taxonomy:** Each mapped organisation is classified by its current recruitment status prior to Phase 3 outreach:
   - `Tier 1: Interviewed` (0 entries at Milestone 2; populated only after informed consent, completed interview conduct, and cross-hub record verification during Phase 3);
   - `Tier 2: Warm Access` (18 entries: direct institutional or professional contact held by the research teams in Buenos Aires, Lagos, Nairobi, or Barcelona);
   - `Tier 3: Referred` (18 entries: named introduction pathway through a first-degree industry, cooperative, legal, or academic contact);
   - `Tier 4: Desk-Identified` (23 entries: verified public agency, regulatory directorate, or commercial operator with a documented consultation channel, awaiting formal outreach);
   - `Tier 5: Hypothetical` (5 entries: central-government tender authority or central-bank gatekeeper included for structural completeness where 12-24 month pilot reachability is low).
2. **Six-Role Institutional Value-Chain Coverage (`R1` to `R6`):** Moving a regional use case from concept to pilot requires alignment across six institutional functions:
   - `R1: Decision Sponsor` (ministry directorate, municipal secretariat, cooperative board, export union executive);
   - `R2: Regulatory Gatekeeper` (central bank, securities commission, data protection authority, specialist legal counsel);
   - `R3: Procurement / Budget Owner` (public procurement agency, municipal innovation fund, humanitarian grant owner);
   - `R4: Local Delivery Partner` (system integrator, software cooperative, agritech or civic-tech engineering team);
   - `R5: Distribution / Trust Partner` (agricultural cooperative federation, SME trade association, public university network, civic federation);
   - `R6: Technical / Compliance Enabler` (licensed VASP/PSAV off-ramp, USSD and mobile-money aggregator, biometric KYC verification provider).
3. **Non-Cardano Counterweights:** Each advancing regional slate includes at least two high-priority **Non-Cardano Counterweights** — commercial operators or institutional platforms currently running on competing chains (Tron TRC-20, Polygon, Celo MiniPay with 20M+ wallets, ZKsync Era) or established Web2 payment and identity systems (NIBSS NIP, NIMC NIN VSPs, M-Pesa, Transferencias 3.0, Decidim). Interviewing these operators tests directly whether Cardano offers a measurable cost, compliance, or auditability advantage over existing infrastructure.
4. **Independence Balance and Conflict-of-Interest Disclosure:** Of the 64 mapped organisations, 52 (81.25%) are independent non-Cardano institutions (`coi_flag: false`) and 12 (18.75%) hold prior Cardano, Wada, or Cardano-adjacent ties (`coi_flag: true`). Three independent institutions first met at CATS26 (`CP-WA-08`, `CP-EA-06`, `CP-EA-08`) are tagged `CATS26-WARM` (`coi_flag: false`, subject to introducer-interviewer separation). During Phase 3 fieldwork, Cardano-affiliated participants (`coi_flag: true`) are capped at 25% of completed interviews (target `<= 20%`), with `Category 7` entries covering external regional builders and independent implementers.
5. **Institutional Archetype Framing Prior to Consent:** Under Milestone 1 [Ethics](../milestone-1/research-design-document/ethics.md) and applicable data-protection laws (NDPA 2023, Kenya DPA 2019, Argentina Law 25.326, EU GDPR), no individual's personal name or private contact details are published here. Entries record the institutional archetype, regulatory mandate, and representative organisational examples; named institutions shown in `Tier 2: Warm Access` and `Tier 3: Referred` rows illustrate the target archetype rather than confirmed consented participants until Phase 3 consent is recorded.

## 4. Phase 3 Recruitment, Substitution, and Quality-Control Rules

During Phase 3 fieldwork across the three advancing emerging-market anchor hubs (Buenos Aires Hub, Nairobi Hub, and Lagos Hub — 3 Local Project Teams per hub = 9 Local Teams in Weeks 7–12), field teams apply four operational rules from Milestone 1 [Stakeholder Recruitment Plan](../milestone-1/research-design-document/stakeholder-recruitment-plan.md):

1. **Regional Interview Quotas (`>= 30` Contractual Floor; 42-51 Target Range Across 3 Hubs / 9 Local Teams):**
   - **Priority 1 — Buenos Aires Hub** (Southern Cone — 3 Local Teams anchoring Argentina, Uruguay, and Paraguay): 14-17 completed interviews across at least four Question Pack categories;
   - **Priority 2 — Nairobi Hub** (East Africa — 3 Local Teams anchoring Nairobi, Central Kenya export cooperatives, and Kigali/Kampala EAC corridors): 13-16 completed interviews across at least four Question Pack categories;
   - **Priority 3 — Lagos Hub** (West Africa — 3 Local Teams anchoring Lagos, Abuja, Northern Nigeria, and the paired Accra/Ghana corridor): 15-18 completed interviews (matching the 14-16 two-play core of peer hubs plus 1-2 diagnostic `H3` calls) across at least four Question Pack categories;
   - **Monitor Clusters** (Iberia / Spain - Barcelona, Brazil, Andean, Mexico): 0 Phase 3 interviews (held at the desk monitoring stage alongside the three active emerging-market fieldwork hubs).
2. **Same-Archetype Substitution Protocol (7-Day Response Window):**
   - Across the three advancing fieldwork hubs, 42 mapped institutional archetypes (14 per hub, alongside 22 desk-monitoring and diagnostic entries in non-advancing clusters = 64 total) each represent a multi-organisation category from which multiple named institutions can be approached during Wave 1 outreach (starting Week 7 for long-lead `Tier 4` regulators) to meet the 42-51 target interview range (30-45 contractual minimum). If a `Tier 2 (Warm Access)` or `Tier 3 (Referred)` organisation does not confirm scheduling within seven calendar days of initial outreach in Weeks 7-8, the regional hub lead substitutes an alternate named institution matching the same functional role (`R1`-`R6`), Question Pack category (1-7), and independence classification (`coi_flag: false`) within the same cluster.
3. **Completion of Non-Cardano Counterweight Interviews:**
   - Before closing its Phase 3 sample, each priority hub must complete interviews with at least two designated Non-Cardano Counterweights: a registered Argentine PSAV and an agricultural export cooperative in the Southern Cone; a coffee or tea export cooperative and an agritech ERP integrator in East Africa; and a capitalised Nigerian SEC-licensed exchange and a biometric KYC aggregator in West Africa.
4. **Role Segregation and Cross-Hub Verification:**
   - Where a participant is introduced through a regional hub or ecosystem network (`PRISMA-HUB`, `CATS26-WARM`, `WADA-NET`, or `CARDANO-ECO`), the team member who made the introduction cannot act as sole interviewer or verify the resulting record. Every Phase 3 interview record undergoes independent cross-hub verification under [Data Ingestion Schema and Fieldwork Verification Protocol](./phase-2-schema-and-protocol-update.md).
