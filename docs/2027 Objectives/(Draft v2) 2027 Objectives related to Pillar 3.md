# (Draft v2) 2027 Objectives related to [Pillar 3 (Governance)](https://product.cardano.intersectmbo.org/vision/strategy-2030/#pillar-3-governance)
Cardano governance must be hard to capture, easy to use, and paced. This builds directly on Cardano’s tripartite model of DReps, Constitutional Committee, and SPOs.

# G.1. Incentivized & Accessible Governance

Encourage broad and meaningful governance participation through activity-based incentives for DReps, SPOs, and Constitutional Committee members. Improve accessibility through tools that simplify delegation, voting, proposal submission, and communication.

Add between 1 to 3 objectives (add all 3 only if it is really needed)

|  | Objective — What should improve in 2027? | Why does it matter? | How could we assess progress? | Domain | Other supported Pillars |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Objective 1 — Accessible & Reliable Governance Participation | Improve the accessibility, reliability and usability of Cardano's governance participation mechanisms, enabling DReps, SPOs, CC members and ADA holders to participate effectively in governance without unnecessary technical or operational barriers, including the cold-key and notification problems that keep SPOs from voting. | Broad and meaningful governance participation depends on reliable, accessible mechanisms for delegation, voting, proposal submission and communication. Technical complexity, unavailable tools or inadequate maintenance can discourage participation and concentrate governance activity among technically experienced stakeholders. | Essential governance functions remain accessible (achievement): Availability of core registration, delegation, voting and governance action functions through maintained tools.  Governance participation becomes easier (progress): Time, steps and successful completion rates for common activities.  Governance services become more reliable (progress): Availability, continuity and maintenance coverage of essential governance tools.  User experience improves (progress): User satisfaction and reduction in reported participation barriers. Participants know when their vote is needed (progress): governance tools and explorers show which bodies must vote on each action. Concentrated voting power and inconsistent engagement can weaken representation, increase governance capture risks and reduce confidence in collective decision-making. **The quality of governance decisions also depends on how participants weigh evidence and draw on relevant domain expertise.** More meaningful participation, a healthier distribution of voting power and **better-reasoned decisions** strengthen the legitimacy and resilience of decentralised governance.  |  Voting power becomes more distributed (progress): the number of DReps needed for a majority, plus the Gini coefficient. Pillar 4 — C.2 Global Engagement & Market Adoption (governance awareness and community engagement). |

# G.2. Turnout-Aware Voting with Delegator Safeguard

Introduce voting mechanisms that account for participation levels to ensure governance decisions reflect meaningful community support. Strengthen delegator protections by enabling ADA holders to override DRep votes on individual Layer 2 decisions without changing delegation.

Add between 1 to 3 objectives (add all 3 only if it is really needed)

|  | Objective — What should improve in 2027? | Why does it matter? | How could we assess progress? | Domain | Other supported Pillars |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Objective 1 — Governance Decision Resilience | Strengthen the resilience and representativeness of Cardano's governance decisions by advancing mechanisms that account for voter participation and provide ADA holders with greater control over their delegated voting power. | Governance decisions should reflect meaningful participation and protect against situations where low turnout or concentrated representation disproportionately influences outcomes. Greater delegator control and turnout-aware decision mechanisms can strengthen governance legitimacy, resilience and trust. | Turnout-aware mechanisms are validated (progress): Research, modelling or evaluation demonstrating how proposed mechanisms address low-turnout governance risks.  Delegator safeguards advance (progress): Technical feasibility and community validation of mechanisms providing greater delegator control.  Implementation readiness improves (progress): Evidence of community support, technical feasibility and a credible path towards implementation.  **Coordination becomes more distributed (progress).** Track which participants (institutions, committees and individuals) have the authority and capacity to perform essential coordination functions independently. Identify functions that depend on a single actor and report whether those dependencies decrease over time. **Social governance incident readiness improves (progress).** Institutions, committees and individuals help define response standards, document plausible scenarios such as coordinated manipulation, and agree on monitoring, roles and communication channels. At least one exercise in 2027 tests whether independent participants can access the evidence, scrutinise decisions and step in if a coordinator is unavailable or has a conflict of interest. Publish the findings. If no scenario warrants a response plan, publish the reasons. TSC view: these mechanisms require ledger changes, so 2027 progress should be a feasibility assessment and draft specification ready for era scoping, with the TSC involved from the start. | Governance Coordination | Pillar 1 — I.1 Scalability & Interoperability (protocol implementation and technical capabilities).  Pillar 1 — I.2 Security & Resilience (governance-related resilience, where technically relevant). |

# G.3. Treasury Seasons

Establish structured annual governance cycles for treasury and budget proposals to improve coordination and decision-making. Increase transparency through regular reporting while reducing proposal overload and governance fatigue.

Add between 1 to 3 objectives (add all 3 only if it is really needed)

|  | Objective — What should improve in 2027? | Why does it matter? | How could we assess progress? | Domain | Other supported Pillars |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Objective 1 — Effective & Transparent Treasury Governance | Improve the predictability, coordination and transparency of Cardano's treasury governance by establishing more structured funding cycles that support informed decision-making, public accountability and effective participation. | Predictable and transparent treasury governance enables participants to plan, evaluate and make funding decisions more effectively. Better coordination can reduce duplicated efforts, proposal overload and governance fatigue, while improving accountability for treasury resources and confidence in funding decisions.  | Treasury governance becomes more predictable (progress): Publication of and adherence to a clearly communicated treasury governance calendar.  Funding decisions become better coordinated (progress): Reduction in overlapping, fragmented or unnecessarily duplicated funding processes.  Treasury transparency improves (progress): Availability, completeness and timeliness of public reporting on approved allocations, withdrawals and remaining balances.  Participation and accountability improve (progress): Engagement in treasury decisions and ability to track funding allocations and reported outcomes.  | Governance Coordination | Pillar 5 — E.1 Financial Stewardship & Tokenomics (treasury asset management, financial accountability and sustainability). |
| Objective 2 \- Strengthen Treasury Accountability | Make the assessment and oversight of funded Treasury withdrawals more transparent, better documented and less dependent on any single institution. Qualified administrators, auditors, milestone reviewers and subject-matter experts should be able to compete for oversight assignments under published selection criteria, with comparable access to the records each assignment requires.  | For any material funding decision, DReps and the community should be able to establish what was promised, which criteria applied, who examined the evidence, why a payment was authorised and how any objections were resolved. That record enables another qualified reviewer to test the reasoning and identify where a different conclusion is warranted. When the same institution defines the standards, selects the reviewers and controls access to the evidence, even capable external specialists have limited scope to challenge its conclusions. Documented procedures, access to evidence and a choice of qualified providers make it possible to obtain a second assessment without relying on the institution whose judgement is under review. That strengthens accountability and reduces the risk of capture.  | Establish a 2026 baseline and compare results across administrators and budget cycles   **Transparency and documentation.** Sample funded Treasury withdrawals to measure how often an independent reviewer can trace a milestone payment from original commitment and acceptance criteria through delivery evidence, assessment, and payment authorisation. Log any breaks in the trail and whether they were resolved.  **Oversight access and concentration.** Track how administration, audit, and review work, and fees, are distributed across providers, noting affiliations to reflect actual independence. Verify that qualified newcomers can identify opportunities, compete under published criteria, and access necessary records.  **Independent scrutiny and follow-through.** Record who developed and applied evaluation standards, and how independent experts could challenge them. Track whether documented objections prompted a reasoned response, leading to a revised standard, corrected decision, or documented rationale for retaining the original.  | Governance Coordination | Pillar 5 \- Ecosystem Sustainability & Resilience, E.1 \- Financial Stewardship & Tokenomics. |

# Additional Section: Institutional Continuity

Keep Cardano’s governance institutions working without interruption: a Constitutional Committee renewed on time, and a Constitution that can be amended through a clear, published process. Not a Cardano 2030 Strategy section today; proposed as the foundation G.1 and G.3 depend on.

Add between 1 to 3 objectives (add all 3 only if it is really needed)

|  | Objective — What should improve in 2027? | Why does it matter? | How could we assess progress? | Domain | Other supported Pillars |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Objective 1 \- Constitutional Committee Continuity | Maintain the continuity and effective functioning of Cardano's Constitutional Committee by ensuring timely, transparent and legitimate renewal of its membership in accordance with constitutional requirements and that members have the support they need to do the job. | The Constitutional Committee is a core institution in Cardano's governance system. Failure to maintain its constitutionally required membership could disrupt constitutional governance and the processing of important decisions. | Required membership is maintained: Constitutional Committee membership remains compliant with applicable requirements.  Renewal is completed on time: Required elections and committee updates are completed before relevant membership expiry.  Renewal is transparent and verifiable: Election and appointment processes follow applicable procedures and provide publicly accessible results. Members are supported (progress): a reimbursement plan covers secure hardware, legal setup and expert advice. A CC compensation model is proposed alongside DRep compensation. Members have a non-CLI way to vote. Outgoing members hand over to incoming ones. | Governance Coordination | Pillar 3 — G.1 Incentivized & Accessible Governance (institutional participation and governance continuity). |
| Objective 2 \- Constitutional Amendment Readiness | Maintain an accessible, transparent and reliable process for proposing, reviewing and deciding constitutional amendments, enabling Cardano's governance system to respond effectively to evolving needs. | Cardano must be able to evolve its constitutional framework when required. Unavailable or unclear amendment processes could delay important governance decisions and create dependencies for protocol development. | Amendment processes remain available: Clearly documented and accessible procedures are maintained. Proposals can be processed effectively: Submitted amendments can progress through the applicable procedures. Processes remain transparent: Proposals, consultations and decisions are publicly accessible. Avoidable delays are minimised: No material delays attributable to missing procedural coordination. Guardrails keep pace with protocol changes (TSC suggestion): guardrails for every new protocol parameter proposed and put to governance before mainnet enactment; TSC with the Parameter Committee. | Governance Coordination | Pillar 3 — G.1 Incentivized & Accessible Governance (accessible governance processes).  Pillar 3 — G.3 Treasury Seasons (procedural coordination where relevant). |
| Objective 3 \- Governance Coordination Continuity | **Strengthen** the operational capacity and coordination needed for Cardano’s governance processes to function reliably, transparently and without avoidable interruption. Enable individuals, independent groups, governance institutions and committees to participate in and scrutinize those processes and to coordinate a response when their integrity is at risk. | Participants in Cardano governance depend on ongoing coordination, facilitation and administrative support. Without operational continuity, essential activities may be interrupted, weakening the effectiveness and reliability of Cardano governance. Beyond MVG identifies **coordinated manipulation** as a governance risk. To reduce the risk of capture, ecosystem-wide coordination should involve multiple independent participants from the outset, including when setting standards and designing incident response plans. Those participants need equitable access to evidence and clear mandates to scrutinise decisions and act if a coordinator is unavailable or has a conflict of interest. | Essential coordination remains available: Core governance support functions operate without material interruption. Governance processes are delivered: Required meetings, elections and decision-making processes are supported when needed.  Operational accountability is maintained: Public reporting on coordination activities and expenditure. Continuity is preserved: No material disruption caused by inadequate operational support. Governance incidents are prepared for (progress): likely incident scenarios are documented, with response roles and communication channels agreed. At least one exercise runs in 2027\. If no scenario needs a response plan, that decision is published with the reasons. | Governance Coordination | Pillar 3 — G.1 Incentivized & Accessible Governance.  Pillar 3 — G.3 Treasury Seasons. |
