## E.1. Financial Stewardship & Tokenomics

Strengthen Cardano's financial sustainability through multi-asset treasury capabilities, active treasury management, and strategic capital deployment. Improve ecosystem tokenomics to ensure sustainable economic incentives and value retention between L2 solutions and the L1 network.

Add between 1 to 3 objectives (add all 3 only if it is really needed)

|  | Objective — What should improve in 2027? | Why does it matter? | How could we assess progress? | Domain | Other supported Pillars |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Objective 1 — Multi-Asset Treasury Capabilities | Expand Cardano's treasury capabilities to support the receipt, management and distribution of assets beyond ADA, increasing financial flexibility and enabling broader participation in treasury funding. | A treasury limited to ADA has less flexibility in receiving, holding and distributing resources. Multi-asset capabilities could enable contributions from Cardano-based applications, support more flexible funding arrangements and provide the foundation for treasury diversification. These capabilities can strengthen financial resilience and reduce operational dependence on a single asset. | Multi-asset treasury capabilities advance (progress): Verified progress towards the ability to receive, hold and distribute non-ADA assets.  Treasury integration improves (progress): Availability of accounting, reporting and governance mechanisms supporting multiple assets. Pillar 3 — G.3 Treasury Seasons (governance processes supporting treasury decisions and withdrawals). |
| Objective 2 — Sustainable Treasury Management | Improve the long-term sustainability of Cardano's treasury through responsible asset management, greater financial resilience and effective deployment of capital that generates measurable economic value while managing risk. | Cardano's long-term development depends on maintaining sufficient treasury resources to support ecosystem priorities. Responsible asset management, financial diversification and risk-aware capital deployment can preserve resources, improve resilience and potentially generate additional income. Transparent financial accountability is essential to ensuring that treasury resources are used effectively and sustainably.  | Treasury financial resilience improves (progress): Diversification, liquidity and exposure to financial risks. Pillar 3 — G.3 Treasury Seasons (transparent governance and oversight of treasury funding decisions). |
| Objective 3 — Sustainable Network Economics | Strengthen Cardano's long-term economic sustainability by increasing recurring network revenue and improving mechanisms that retain economic value within the Layer 1 ecosystem, including from Layer 2 activity. | Cardano's long-term security and development depend on sustainable economic incentives and recurring network revenue. As activity increases and Layer 2 solutions expand, ensuring that economic value contributes to Layer 1 becomes increasingly important. More sustainable network economics can reduce reliance on monetary expansion and support long-term network security and financial resilience. | Recurring protocol revenue increases (progress): Revenue generated through L1 transaction fees and other protocol-level mechanisms. Network economic sustainability improves (progress): Progress towards a more sustainable balance between recurring network revenue and ongoing economic obligations.  Tokenomics mechanisms improve (progress): Evidence that network economic mechanisms support sustainable incentives, competitive usage and value retention. | Applied Research | Pillar 1 — I.1 Scalability & Interoperability (L2 integration and settlement capabilities).  Pillar 2 — A.1 High-Value Verticals (increased economic activity generating network demand and transaction fees).  Pillar 5 — E.2 SPO Incentives (sustainable economics supporting network operators).  |

## E.2. SPO Incentives

Expand economic opportunities for stake pool operators through participation in L2 infrastructure, additional validation services, and decentralized hosting. Preserve network security and decentralization by maintaining a broad, economically sustainable distribution of independent stake pools.

Add between 1 to 3 objectives (add all 3 only if it is really needed)

|  | Objective — What should improve in 2027? | Why does it matter? | How could we assess progress? | Domain | Other supported Pillars |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Objective 1 — SPO Economic Sustainability & Decentralisation | Improve the economic viability and resilience of independent stake pool operators while preserving Cardano's decentralisation and network security, including by expanding opportunities for operators to generate sustainable revenue from additional network services. | Independent SPOs are fundamental to Cardano's security, decentralisation and operational continuity. Increasing infrastructure costs, evolving technical requirements and changing staking economics may make it harder for smaller independent operators to remain viable. Supporting sustainable operating economics and additional revenue opportunities can help maintain a broad and resilient network of independent stake pools. | Independent SPOs become more economically sustainable (progress): Operating costs, rewards and net operating margins for representative independent operators. Independent operator retention improves (progress): Share of independent SPOs remaining active over time, including those facing economic pressure.  Additional revenue opportunities become viable (progress): Number of operators generating sustainable net income from additional network services.  Operators remain capable of meeting technical requirements (progress): Ability of independent SPOs to meet evolving infrastructure requirements without unsustainable financial pressure. | Open Contribution | Pillar 1 — I.2 Security & Resilience (maintaining a secure, decentralised and resilient network).  Pillar 1 — I.1 Scalability & Interoperability (technical upgrades affecting SPO infrastructure requirements and opportunities to support additional services). |
