# (Draft) 2027 Objectives related to [Pillar 3 (Governance)](https://product.cardano.intersectmbo.org/vision/strategy-2030/#pillar-3-governance)
Cardano governance must be hard to capture, easy to use, and paced. This builds directly on Cardano’s tripartite model of DReps, Constitutional Committee, and SPOs.

# G.1. Incentivized & Accessible Governance

Encourage broad and meaningful governance participation through activity-based incentives for DReps, SPOs, and Constitutional Committee members. Improve accessibility through tools that simplify delegation, voting, proposal submission, and communication.

Establish structured annual governance cycles for treasury and budget proposals to improve coordination and decision-making. Increase transparency through regular reporting while reducing proposal overload and governance fatigue.

# Additional Section: Institutional Continuity

Keep Cardano’s governance institutions working without interruption: a Constitutional Committee renewed on time, and a Constitution that can be amended through a clear, published process. Not a Cardano 2030 Strategy section today; proposed as the foundation G.1 and G.3 depend on.

Add between 1 to 3 objectives (add all 3 only if it is really needed)

|  | Objective — What should improve in 2027? | Why does it matter? | How could we assess progress? | Domain | Other supported Pillars |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Objective 1 \- Constitutional Committee Continuity | Maintain the continuity and effective functioning of Cardano's Constitutional Committee by ensuring timely, transparent and legitimate renewal of its membership in accordance with constitutional requirements. | The Constitutional Committee is a core institution in Cardano's governance system. Failure to maintain its constitutionally required membership could disrupt constitutional governance and the processing of important decisions. | Required membership is maintained: Constitutional Committee membership remains compliant with applicable requirements.  Renewal is completed on time: Required elections and committee updates are completed before relevant membership expiry.  Renewal is transparent and verifiable: Election and appointment processes follow applicable procedures and provide publicly accessible results. | Governance Coordination | Pillar 3 — G.1 Incentivized & Accessible Governance (institutional participation and governance continuity). |
| Objective 2 \- Constitutional Amendment Readiness | Maintain an accessible, transparent and reliable process for proposing, reviewing and deciding constitutional amendments, enabling Cardano's governance system to respond effectively to evolving needs. | Cardano must be able to evolve its constitutional framework when required. Unavailable or unclear amendment processes could delay important governance decisions and create dependencies for protocol development. | Amendment processes remain available: Clearly documented and accessible procedures are maintained. Proposals can be processed effectively: Submitted amendments can progress through the applicable procedures. Processes remain transparent: Proposals, consultations and decisions are publicly accessible. Governance processes are delivered: Required meetings, elections and decision-making processes are supported when needed. Operational accountability is maintained: Public reporting on coordination activities and expenditure. Continuity is preserved: No material disruption caused by inadequate operational support. | Governance Coordination | Pillar 3 — G.1 Incentivized & Accessible Governance.  Pillar 3 — G.3 Treasury Seasons. |
