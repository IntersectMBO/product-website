ASISA — Project Inception and Timeline

Chapter 9: Project Inception, Workspace, Cadence and Timeline​

9.1 Project Kickoff​

The formal project kickoff was held in August 2026. At the kickoff the research team confirmed alignment with the RFP 05 scope and the CPC's strategic objectives; confirmed the nine decision gates as the governing evidence framework; established the progress-reporting cadence with the CPC (bi-weekly progress updates per Contract Appendix A, Section 2); and confirmed the digital workspace.

9.2 Progress and Timeline Note​

[!NOTE] Why this submission follows the Symposium: The inception period coincided with a convergence of stakeholder access opportunities, principally The Actuarial and Insurance Symposium 2.0. The research team made a deliberate decision to align the inception phase with that event, and not to submit a perfunctory inception report on the original schedule and then seek stakeholder access through cold outreach.

That decision produced warm access to twelve tier-one institutions across the regulatory, underwriting, Takaful, insurtech and professional-body segments (Chapter 4), together with an event partner (NADP). In addition, the research team executed the Milestone 2 desk research, preliminary regulatory scan, ecosystem mapping, and Gate A provisional screen in parallel during the pre-approval window so that Milestone 2 is ready for immediate review upon Milestone 1 sign-off. The fieldwork phase (Phase 4) can therefore begin immediately from established relationships, compressing the remaining post-approval execution cycle into 10 project weeks (Weeks 1 to 10, corresponding to Contractual Weeks 15 to 24) to meet the contractual completion date of 8 January 2027. It also allowed the research design to be finalised with an accurate access roster and with the regulatory developments of 2025 and 2026 (§1.2) reflected in it.

This submission records the alignment formally for the CPC's information.

9.4 Post-Approval Project Timeline (10 Weeks to 8 January 2027 Contractual Completion)​

Weeks count from formal Milestone 1 approval by the CPC (see the baseline reconciliation and operational dependency note below and §3.3).

[!NOTE] Project-Week Baseline Reconciliation & Operational Dependencies (8 January 2027 Contractual Deadline): Under Contract Appendix A (commenced 24 July 2026), the 24-week contractual project window runs from 24 July 2026 to 8 January 2027. Because pre-approval inception, the 26 August 2026 Actuarial and Insurance Symposium 2.0, and the parallel execution of Milestone 2 desk research, statutory scanning, ecosystem mapping, and Gate A provisional screening spanned the first 14 contractual weeks (24 July to late October 2026), the remaining post-approval execution window comprises 10 project weeks (Weeks 1 to 10 from formal Milestone 1 approval, corresponding to Contractual Weeks 15 to 24), completing on the contractual Milestone 5 deadline of 8 January 2027. This reconciles Chapter 3 (§3.3), Chapter 4 (§4.6), and Chapter 9 directly with Contract Appendix A without requiring a contract extension.

Operational Note on Fieldwork Compression & Review/Disbursement Dependencies: Because each milestone is methodologically gated on the locked instruments of the preceding milestone (and Milestone Acceptance Forms cannot be lodged until PR review sign-off), primary fieldwork (Phase 4, Milestone 3) cannot commence prior to Milestone 1 and Milestone 2 sign-off. In the Nigerian institutional environment, converting even warm regulatory and underwriting relationships into completed interviews requires scheduling lead time and physical, in-person field travel across Northern Nigeria (Zaria, Kaduna, Abuja) and Southern Nigeria (Lagos) before the mid-December corporate and public-sector holiday slowdown. Completing Phases 4 and 5 within Weeks 1 to 6 (by mid-December 2026) so that Phases 6 to 8 conclude by 8 January 2027 is therefore contingent on expedited review sign-off and timely milestone payment disbursement for Milestone 1 and Milestone 2 (already submitted in parallel under PR #75) to fund inter-state field logistics.
