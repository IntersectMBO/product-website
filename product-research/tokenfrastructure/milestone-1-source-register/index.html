Use and quality controls​

This register converts the inception source categories into a specific, reviewable baseline. It names documents, standards, repositories, dashboards, protocols and organisations that will be consulted. Inclusion is not validation: first-party sources establish what a provider or ecosystem claims and must be corroborated before supporting an independent conclusion.

  • Official technical specifications and public-authority sources receive the greatest weight for what they directly establish.
  • Product pages, launch articles, roadmaps and provider metrics are treated as first-party evidence and labelled accordingly.
  • Dynamic dashboards retain their observation period and denominator; transactions are not treated as users, revenue or adoption.
  • Draft standards are assessed as proposals, not as established market practice.
  • New sources may be added where needed to test a material claim, but this list is the Milestone 1 baseline.

The source IDs are retained from the working credibility ledger. S01 and S02 identify the governing agreement and proposal in the controlled project record; the relevant Milestone 1 obligations are reproduced in the Milestone 1 overview and are not treated as external research evidence.
