How to read this list​

This organisation-level longlist makes the Milestone 1 interview plan actionable while protecting individual privacy. Confirmed target means that the project has fixed the organisation as part of the initial recruitment universe; it does not mean that the organisation or any individual has agreed to participate.

  • No personal names, email addresses, consent records or outreach status are published here.
  • Individual identities and participation status are maintained in a controlled respondent log.
  • Inclusion does not imply participation, endorsement or a commitment to interview all 50 organisations.
  • The longlist is approximately twice the maximum intended sample, providing substitutions for non-response without activating the excluded extended-interview add-on.
  • Visa and Kaleido are excluded from outreach at this stage. Visa may still appear in the separate public-source register as a comparison source; source review is not respondent outreach.
  • Interviews will begin with the respondent's problems, existing controls and decision criteria. Cardano will not be presented as the assumed solution.

Recruitment and evidence controls​

  • Respondents will receive a neutral study introduction and may choose a conversation or written response.
  • Participation, attribution and recording require consent; non-participation will not be disclosed.
  • Questions will seek concrete examples, evidence and counterexamples rather than agreement with a proposed Cardano narrative.
  • Vendor and Cardano-connected statements will be marked as such until independently corroborated.
  • The Milestone 2 interview log will report realised coverage and evidence status without exposing confidential personal information.
