Summary​

The goal of this milestone was to understand what is the current state of the ecosystem in terms in strategy and objectives. Making sure to understand what work is currently being done in terms of BD, what works, what doesn't, and why we need a more coordinated approach. Also analyzing what is being done in other blockchain ecosystems, and starting to develop the initial Impact Vertical Chain Template.

Issues and Blockers​

  • We encountered issues mainly in the actual strategy, and understanding how things would be managed, planned or funded. Who should be eligible or not to lead a vertical, should it be a person or a team.
  • A lot of questionning around the management of all of this, should there be an entity leading all the verticals, where should the vertical leads report to. Should there already be initial work into the vertical before asking for funding ?
  • We tried answering these questions thanks to the different discussions we have had with different Cardano actors, builders, DReps. We have not all the answers yet but it will be refined when completing milestone 2.

Plan for Next Milestone​

Phase 2 — Framework Design & Expert Validation (Weeks 4–8)

  • Conduct 10–12 structured interviews with vertical leads, dApp builders, BD professionals, and DReps across the Cardano ecosystem
  • Iterate the framework based on expert input, stress-testing the Impact Vertical Chain template against multiple hypothetical verticals
  • Validate the Bottleneck & Enabler Taxonomy with practitioners from at least 3 different ecosystem domains
  • Host a live community workshop (Week 7) presenting the draft framework to ~30 community members for open feedback
  • Finalize and publish the complete framework documentation on Intersect platform

This work should be completed by the end of September.

Here are the deliverables:

  • Impact Vertical Chain Template Fully documented, reusable template for mapping any vertical's activities to Cardano 2030 KPIs
  • Standardized scenario modeling methodology with shared input variables and KPI output metrics
  • Classification system for vertical adoption constraints and accelerators, validated with ecosystem practitioners
  • One-page self-service planning and reporting tool for vertical leads
  • Full documentation of framework design rationale, validation process, expert insights, and usage guide
